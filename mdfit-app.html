The MD Fit App

★★★★★ 5.0 · 89 Google reviews

The MD Fit app puts the same exercise videos, programs and pain tool Dr. Jared uses with his clinic patients on your phone. You do not need to live in Sarasota or book a visit to start, and it is free to sign up.

Open the app

Why it exists

Most of what fixes a persistent pain or a stalled lift happens between appointments, not during them. In the clinic, every plan ends the same way: assess, release, correct, then assign homework and build strength. The homework is where the change actually sticks, and for years it lived on paper handouts that got lost in a car.

The app is that homework, built properly. Clinic patients use it between visits. And because the programs and videos stand on their own, people who will never set foot on Clark Road use it too: someone in another state with a cranky hip, a lifter trying to fix their squat, a retiree rebuilding strength after a long break.

What is inside

  • The pain tool. Tap where it hurts on the body map. It shows the muscles that most often refer pain to that spot, how to check each one, and a short exercise track to start with. It is the same reasoning behind the muscle library and the pain-location pages on this site.
  • The exercise video library. Close to two hundred exercises filmed by Dr. Jared: mobility, rehab, strength, power, activation and desk and posture work, each sorted by body region and level.
  • Programs. Multi-week plans for specific goals, from rehab protocols to sport preparation for golf, pickleball, running and lifting, laid out session by session.
  • The workout generator. Choose a goal, where you are training and your level, and it builds a session from the library for you.
  • Progress tracking. Workouts, pain check-ins and visits on one calendar, a daily streak, and the record you need to see whether things are actually improving.
  • Lectures and live Q&A. Recorded lectures and a monthly live session with Dr. Jared.
  • The MD Fit guide. Tap the cloud and describe what is going on in plain words. It points you to the right part of the app. It is a guide to the app, not medical advice.

Who it is for

Clinic patients between visits. Your homework is in the app, so you are never guessing which exercise came next or how many to do.

People who are not in Sarasota, or not ready to come in. The programs and the pain tool work without a visit. If you want more guidance later, the 1-on-1 tiers add scheduled remote video sessions with Dr. Jared.

People who train. Lifters, runners, golfers and pickleball players who want their training built around how their own body moves. The train for your build pages explain the thinking; the app is where you put it into practice.

Older adults rebuilding strength. Progressive, sensible work for bone, muscle and balance, at a pace that respects where you are starting from.

Free, membership and 1-on-1

Creating an account is free. Membership is $19.95 a month and adds your own workout builder and challenges, every premade program, the full lecture library, the monthly live Q&A and detailed progress comparisons.

The Accountability Membership is $29.95 a month and takes $1 off for every day you log a workout or a pain check-in. Log 20 active days and that month costs $10. It rewards exactly the consistency that makes the work pay off.

The 1-on-1 tiers add scheduled remote video sessions with Dr. Jared each month, the option to message him directly, and a program built for you. You can see every option and manage your membership from your profile in the app.

When to come in instead

The app does not examine you and it does not diagnose. It is built around common, mechanical, muscle-related problems and around training well. Some situations need a person in the room first, and some need a physician before anyone else.

Book an evaluation if a pain is not settling after a few weeks of sensible work, if you are not sure what is going on, or if you are recovering from surgery and want a plan built around your surgeon's protocol. See a physician first for anything on the warning-sign list below.

How to get it

The app runs in your phone or computer's web browser, so there is nothing to find in an app store and nothing that needs updating. Open the app, create your free account, then add it to your home screen so it opens like any other app:

  • iPhone: open it in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Computer: it works in any modern browser; in Chrome or Edge you can install it from the address bar.

Already a clinic patient? Sign in with the same email you booked with and your visits and documents will be there.

See a physician first if

  • Chest pain, pressure or shortness of breath, especially with exertion: call 911.
  • New weakness, numbness in the groin or saddle area, or loss of bladder or bowel control: go to an emergency department now.
  • A sudden, severe headache unlike any before, or headache with vision change or confusion: call 911.
  • Pain after a fall or impact with swelling, deformity or inability to bear weight: see a physician the same day.
  • Fever, night sweats or unexplained weight loss alongside pain: see a physician before starting any program.

Frequently asked questions

Is the MD Fit app free?

Creating an account is free. Membership is $19.95 a month and unlocks the full set of programs, your own workout builder, the lecture library and the monthly live Q&A.

Do I need to live in Sarasota to use it?

No. The pain tool, the exercise videos and the programs work from anywhere. The 1-on-1 tiers add remote video sessions with Dr. Jared, so you can get personal guidance without traveling.

Does the app replace an evaluation?

No. It does not examine or diagnose you. It is a strong place to start for common muscle-related problems and for training, and an evaluation is the right step when a problem is not settling or you are not sure what it is.

Is it on the App Store or Google Play?

It runs in your web browser and installs to your home screen from there, so you do not need an app store. It works the same on iPhone, Android and a computer.

Can I talk to Dr. Jared through the app?

Members can send him messages in the app, and the 1-on-1 tiers add scheduled remote video sessions and direct messaging.
