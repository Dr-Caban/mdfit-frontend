1. Who we are

MD Fit LLC ("MD Fit," "we," "us") provides physical rehabilitation, exercise physiology, medical massage and fitness services led by Dr. Jared Caban, MD, MSc, CEP, at 3214 Clark Road, Sarasota, Florida 34231. This policy covers our website (mdfitsrq.com and mdfit-srq.vercel.app), the MD Fit web and desktop apps, booking, membership, messaging and in-studio services.

The short version: we collect what we need to treat and train you, we encrypt it, we never sell it, and we never use it for advertising.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account and contact details: name, email, phone number and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way scrypt hash, so we can't read them.
  • Booking details: the service, date and time you choose, and any notes you add.
  • Intake and health details: your goals, pain areas, pain ratings, injuries, surgeries and history you share on intake forms or in the app.
  • App activity: pain logs, protocol progress, generated and completed workouts, and home-exercise logs.
  • Vitals: heart rate and similar readings if you choose to connect a Bluetooth heart-rate strap or watch. Readings are used during your session and saved only to your workout log.
  • Messages: what you send Dr. Jared through member messaging.
  • Reviews and newsletter sign-ups: if you leave a review or join our email list.

Payment information

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card number goes straight to Stripe and never touches our servers. We receive only a payment confirmation, the last four digits, card brand, and your subscription or plan status.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, approximate country (to enforce US-only access), browser type and device type, used for security and rate limiting.
  • Security logs: an audit record of sign-ins and access to client records. IP addresses in the audit record are stored as one-way hashes.

We do not use advertising trackers, social-media pixels or third-party analytics that profile you.

3. How we use it

  • Provide your evaluations, sessions, protocols and app features.
  • Schedule appointments and send confirmations, reminders and cancellation links.
  • Process payments, memberships, plans, refunds and the money-back guarantee.
  • Let you message Dr. Jared and notify you when he replies.
  • Track your progress and tailor workouts and protocol guidance.
  • Keep the service secure: block bots, scrapers and abuse, and detect unauthorized access.
  • Send our newsletter, only if you sign up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Meet legal, tax and recordkeeping requirements.

We never sell or rent your personal information and never share it for targeted advertising.

5. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS with HSTS).
  • Stored data is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Messages are encrypted separately.
  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt.
  • Administrator sign-in requires a password plus a one-time code sent by email.
  • Access to client records is written to a tamper-evident, cryptographically signed audit log.
  • Rate limits, bot and scraper blocking, and a US-only access policy reduce automated attacks.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as required by Florida law (Fla. Stat. § 501.171) and any other applicable law.

6. Health information

We treat everything you share about your body, pain, injuries and progress as sensitive and protect it with the safeguards above. MD Fit provides exercise, rehabilitation and fitness services. Depending on the service and how it's paid for, some records may be covered by HIPAA. Where HIPAA applies, our Notice of Privacy Practices also applies; ask us for a copy. Whether or not HIPAA applies, we limit who can see your health information to Dr. Jared and staff who need it for your care.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use only what the site needs to work:

  • Sign-in: a session token kept in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • App data: your browser's local storage and an offline cache, so the app loads fast and some features work offline.
  • Preferences: small settings such as your last-viewed tab.

We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Clearing your browser data or signing out removes these.

8. How long we keep it

We keep account and care records while your account is active and afterward as long as needed for your care, legal and tax obligations, and resolving disputes. Florida generally requires certain health-care records to be kept for several years. Messages keep the most recent history. Security and audit logs are kept to protect record integrity. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: see and update most of your information in the app, or ask us for a copy.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your account. We'll delete what we're not required to keep.
  • Email: unsubscribe from the newsletter any time. Appointment and account emails continue while you use the service.
  • Payments: manage or cancel your membership through "Manage billing" in the app.
  • Devices: heart-rate connections are optional and you can disconnect at any time.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We'll verify your identity and respond within 30 days.

10. US-only service

MD Fit is offered only in the United States and its territories. Access from other countries is blocked, and your information is stored and processed in the United States.

11. Children

Accounts are for adults 18 and over. Minors may receive services only with a parent or guardian, who manages the account. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13 online.

12. Changes to this policy

We'll post updates here and change the effective date above. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
