How the pain finder works

Pain is often felt somewhere other than the muscle causing it. Dr. Jared Caban built this map from the referred-pain patterns he uses in the clinic: tap the area that hurts, choose the picture that looks most like your pain, and the finder lists the muscles that most often send pain there, in order, with a free exercise track for each pattern.

Read more about your pain

Every spot on the map has its own page in the pain-location library, and every muscle it names has a page in the muscle library with symptoms, a self-check, look-alikes and a three-phase relief plan. The exercises are in the exercise library, and full week-by-week plans are on the programs page.

Get checked instead if

  • You have chest pain, shortness of breath, a sudden severe headache, or weakness or numbness on one side of the body: call 911.
  • Pain followed a fall, an accident or a blow.
  • You have new numbness, tingling or weakness, or loss of bladder or bowel control.
  • Pain comes with fever, unexplained weight loss, or is worse at night no matter how you lie.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the pain finder point to a muscle somewhere else?

Muscles often refer pain away from themselves. A tight upper trapezius can cause a headache at the temple, and a gluteus minimus can send pain down the outside of the leg. The map is built from those referral patterns.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It shows which muscles commonly cause pain in the spot you choose, so you know where to start. A hands-on evaluation is what confirms the source.

Do I need an account?

No. The map and the exercise videos it suggests are free, with no sign-up.

Do you take insurance?

No. MD Fit is a self-pay practice and does not bill insurance. You pay at the visit and receive an itemized receipt; no referral is needed. An evaluation is $300 (about 90 minutes) and follow-ups are $150. Paying directly is what lets Dr. Jared spend a full session with you, with no visit caps set by an insurer.
