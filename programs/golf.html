6-Week Beginner Golf Protocol

★★★★★ 5.0 · 89 Google reviews

Improve mobility, build better movement and develop a stronger swing. 3 sessions a week for 6 weeks — mobility first, form always.

Get the full program in the app Book an evaluation in Sarasota

At a glance

  • Length: 5 weeks
  • Level: beginner
  • Where: at home
  • Type: Sport Performance
  • Aim: Improve Mobility. Build Better Movement. Develop A Stronger Swing.

Week by week

  1. Week 1: Foundations. Establish mobility, posture and movement control.
  2. Week 2: Build Movement Range. Improve active mobility, refine control, and connect mobility to golf mechanics.
  3. Week 3: Build Rotational Stability. Integrate mobility with strength and coordination. Develop control through rotation, balance and sequencing.
  4. Week 5: Integrate Movement & Swing. Combine mobility, strength and control. Begin to connect movement patterns to a repeatable golf swing with smooth sequencing.
  5. Week 6: Confidence & Consistency. Bring it all together. Combine mobility, strength and sequencing to perform a repeatable, efficient golf swing with control.

MOBILITY FIRST. FORM ALWAYS.

How week 1 is organized

  • Workout A: Hip mobility and glute engagement; Neutral spine and proper golf posture; Controlled movement through a comfortable range; Quality over speed.
  • Workout B: Thoracic spine mobility; Shoulder control and posture; Upper-body rotation and stability; Maintain a relaxed, athletic upper body.
  • Workout C: Glute activation and hip stability; Lower-body mobility and balance; Control through full range of motion; Proper weight transfer and stance awareness.

Stop and get checked if

  • An exercise causes sharp, shooting or worsening pain, or pain that lasts into the next day.
  • You have new numbness, tingling or weakness.
  • You are recovering from surgery and your surgeon has not cleared you for this kind of exercise.
  • You have chest pain, shortness of breath or a sudden severe headache: call 911.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the full program?

Week 1 is on this page. All 5 weeks, with the videos for every exercise and progress tracking, are in the MD Fit app with a membership. Patients at the Sarasota clinic may be assigned it as part of their plan.

Can I do it without seeing Dr. Jared first?

Many people do, especially with the remote 1-on-1 options in the app. If you have pain you can't explain, recent surgery, or symptoms in the red-flag list, get evaluated first.

What if an exercise hurts?

Stop it. Every MD Fit program works inside a pain-free range; discomfort from effort is fine, sharp or worsening pain is not. Go back to the previous week's version or contact Dr. Jared.
