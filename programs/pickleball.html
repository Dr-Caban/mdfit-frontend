Pickleball Beginner Protocol

★★★★★ 5.0 · 89 Google reviews

Move better, play longer and lower your injury risk. Mobility, stability, strength, power, endurance and court confidence — 3 sessions a week for 6 weeks.

Get the full program in the app Book an evaluation in Sarasota

At a glance

  • Length: 6 weeks
  • Level: beginner
  • Where: at home
  • Type: Sport Performance
  • Aim: Move Better. Play Longer. Lower Injury Risk.

Week by week

  1. Week 1: Movement Foundations. Learn the basics and build control.
  2. Week 2: Stability & Control. Improve balance and single-leg control.
  3. Week 3: Strength Development. Build lower-body and core strength.
  4. Week 4: Movement Integration. Combine strength, balance and movement.
  5. Week 5: Power & Endurance. Increase speed and repeated movement ability.
  6. Week 6: Confidence & Play. Put it all together for consistent, pain-free play.

What to expect

  • Build a strong foundation
  • Improve mobility and stability
  • Learn proper movement patterns
  • Reduce injury risk
  • Play with more confidence

The rules of this program

  • Glutes engaged at all times.
  • Shoulders down (in your pockets).
  • Control the movement.
  • Quality over quantity.
  • Stay balanced and athletic.
  • Move pain-free.

Stop and get checked if

  • An exercise causes sharp, shooting or worsening pain, or pain that lasts into the next day.
  • You have new numbness, tingling or weakness.
  • You are recovering from surgery and your surgeon has not cleared you for this kind of exercise.
  • You have chest pain, shortness of breath or a sudden severe headache: call 911.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the full program?

Week 1 is on this page. All 6 weeks, with the videos for every exercise and progress tracking, are in the MD Fit app with a membership. Patients at the Sarasota clinic may be assigned it as part of their plan.

Can I do it without seeing Dr. Jared first?

Many people do, especially with the remote 1-on-1 options in the app. If you have pain you can't explain, recent surgery, or symptoms in the red-flag list, get evaluated first.

What if an exercise hurts?

Stop it. Every MD Fit program works inside a pain-free range; discomfort from effort is fine, sharp or worsening pain is not. Go back to the previous week's version or contact Dr. Jared.

Do you take insurance?

No. MD Fit is a self-pay practice and does not bill insurance. You pay at the visit and receive an itemized receipt; no referral is needed. An evaluation is $300 (about 90 minutes) and follow-ups are $150. Paying directly is what lets Dr. Jared spend a full session with you, with no visit caps set by an insurer.
