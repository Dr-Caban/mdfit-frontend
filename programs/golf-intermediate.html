6-Week Intermediate Golf Program

★★★★★ 5.0 · 89 Google reviews

Rotational strength, power and control to build on your beginner foundation and develop a stronger, more consistent swing.

Get the full program in the app Book an evaluation in Sarasota

At a glance

  • Length: 6 weeks
  • Level: intermediate
  • Where: a gym
  • Type: Sport Performance
  • Aim: Mobility. Power. Control. A More Efficient Swing.

Week by week

  1. Week 1: Rotational Strength Foundations. Build controlled rotational loading and hip-torso separation.
  2. Week 2: Unilateral Strength & Stability. Improve trail-leg loading and lead-leg control.
  3. Week 3: Rotational Power Development. Convert strength into faster rotational movement.
  4. Week 4: Ground Force & Athletic Movement. Develop coordinated force production.
  5. Week 5: Speed & Power Integration. Combine strength, speed and sequencing.
  6. Week 6: Performance & Repeatability. Express power with control and assess progress.

MOVE BETTER. PLAY BETTER.

How week 1 is organized

  • Workout A: Hip hinge and lower-body strength; Rotational control and power foundation; Connect lower body to upper body; Build strength without losing mobility.
  • Workout B: Single-leg strength and balance; Core stability and anti-rotation control; Dynamic stability for the golf swing; Maintain posture under load.
  • Workout C: Rotational power and sequencing; Explosive lower-body movement; Connect strength to swing mechanics; Improve speed with control.

Stop and get checked if

  • An exercise causes sharp, shooting or worsening pain, or pain that lasts into the next day.
  • You have new numbness, tingling or weakness.
  • You are recovering from surgery and your surgeon has not cleared you for this kind of exercise.
  • You have chest pain, shortness of breath or a sudden severe headache: call 911.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the full program?

Week 1 is on this page. All 6 weeks, with the videos for every exercise and progress tracking, are in the MD Fit app with a membership. Patients at the Sarasota clinic may be assigned it as part of their plan.

Can I do it without seeing Dr. Jared first?

Many people do, especially with the remote 1-on-1 options in the app. If you have pain you can't explain, recent surgery, or symptoms in the red-flag list, get evaluated first.

What if an exercise hurts?

Stop it. Every MD Fit program works inside a pain-free range; discomfort from effort is fine, sharp or worsening pain is not. Go back to the previous week's version or contact Dr. Jared.
