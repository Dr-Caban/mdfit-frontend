Week 1 preview: Foundation & Activation

WALL-SUPPORTED HIP / GLUTE ACTIVATION (5-10 reps reps, hold 1-2 seconds, DAILY (1 session), [object Object])

  1. Stand facing a wall and place your hips high against the wall.
  2. Keep your heels up off the ground.
  3. Keep your knees pretty well extended (soft, not locked).
  4. Turn your knees and toes slightly inward.
  5. Bend forward from the hips (let your torso hang).
  6. Engage and squeeze your glutes.
  7. Hold for 1-2 seconds.
  8. Perform 5-10 repetitions, once daily.
  9. Gluteus Maximus (engage)
  10. Hamstrings (lengthened)

Focus: Hips high on the wall; Heels up off the ground; Knees nearly extended (soft, not locked); Knees and toes slightly inward; Bend forward and relax the upper body; Engage the glutes (squeeze); Hold 1-2 seconds; Repeat 5-10 times daily.

Tip: If this causes increased radiating, burning or tingling pain (nerve-like symptoms), reduce to 1-2 repetitions initially and build tolerance gradually.

PELVIC FLOOR ACTIVATION (5-10 repetitions reps, every time after you urinate, [object Object])

  1. After you urinate, gently contract the muscles to hold urine (bladder Kegel), then relax. Do 5-10 repetitions.
  2. Then contract the muscles to hold fecal matter (anal Kegel), then relax. Do 5-10 repetitions.
  3. Perform both exercises every time after you urinate.
  4. BLADDER (URINARY) KEGEL: Same motion you would use to stop urine flow.
  5. ANAL (BOWEL) KEGEL: Same motion you would use to hold fecal matter.

Focus: Do not repeatedly start and stop your urine stream.; Use the sensation of stopping/expelling as a guide, and perform the repetitions after urination.; Keep the contractions gentle and controlled.; Breathe normally..

Remember: Muscle soreness is acceptable. Joint pain, sharp pain, numbness, tingling, or increasing radiating pain are not.

Stop and get checked if

  • An exercise causes sharp, shooting or worsening pain, or pain that lasts into the next day.
  • You have new numbness, tingling or weakness.
  • You are recovering from surgery and your surgeon has not cleared you for this kind of exercise.
  • You have chest pain, shortness of breath or a sudden severe headache: call 911.

Can I do it without seeing Dr. Jared first?

Many people do, especially with the remote 1-on-1 options in the app. If you have pain you can't explain, recent surgery, or symptoms in the red-flag list, get evaluated first.

What if an exercise hurts?

Stop it. Every MD Fit program works inside a pain-free range; discomfort from effort is fine, sharp or worsening pain is not. Go back to the previous week's version or contact Dr. Jared.
