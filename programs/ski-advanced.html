Advanced Ski Program

An 8-week plan for power, single-leg dominance and reactive stability: stronger ankles, more control, less fatigue, bigger days on snow.

At a glance

  • Length: 8 weeks
  • Level: advanced
  • Where: a gym
  • Type: Sport Performance
  • Aim: Power • Single-Leg Dominance • Reactive Stability

Week by week

  1. Week 1. Week 1 introduces advanced movement patterns with single-leg power, explosive jumping, off-balance loading, and dynamic stability while maintaining essential ankle and knee mobility.
  2. Week 2. Week 2 increases intensity with more dynamic single-leg work, reactive landing and off-balance loads. Build explosive strength, control and resilience for the demands of the mountain.
  3. Week 3. Week 3 builds explosive strength, faster transition control, and off-balance power with more dynamic single-leg work, cable resistance, and multi-planar movement. You'll move more athletically and stay stable under load.
  4. Week 4. Week 4 builds higher force and complexity with off-balance loading, single-leg strength, and reactive power. You'll increase intensity, improve multi-planar control, and develop the ability to absorb and redirect force in unpredictable terrain.
  5. Week 5. Week 5 increases intensity with higher height jumps, more complex off-balance loads, unilateral plyometrics, and multi-directional control. You'll build power endurance and confidence in unpredictable terrain.
  6. Week 6. Week 6 focuses on high-intensity power, maximal single-leg control, complex movement patterns, and ski-specific endurance. You will move faster, react quicker, and stay stable in dynamic, unpredictable terrain.
  7. Week 7. Week 7 peaks your intensity with high-demand single-leg power, complex movement patterns, and ski-specific endurance. You'll move faster, react quicker, and build the resilience to handle variable terrain and longer days on the mountain.
  8. Week 8. Week 8 brings everything together. You'll peak with high-intensity, complex, ski-specific movement, full-body integration, and reactive power. You'll move more efficiently, stay more stable on longer, stronger days on the mountain.

Daily basics

  • Stable Ankles: Absorb terrain and stay in control.
  • Resilient Knees: Handle turns and uneven terrain.
  • Powerful Hips: Create stability and explosive movement.
  • Stronger Core: Transfer force and stay balanced.
  • Ski-Ready Movement: Confidence and endurance for the slopes.

Move better. Play longer. 8-Week Plan GOSD – Glutes on. Shoulders down.

The rules of this program

  • Explode, stick, and control..
  • Knees track over toes..
  • Keep hips level and stable..
  • Land soft and quiet..
  • Use your core, not your lower back..
  • Full range of motion..
  • Quality over quantity..
  • Move with intent, not speed..

Stop and get checked if

  • An exercise causes sharp, shooting or worsening pain, or pain that lasts into the next day.
  • You have new numbness, tingling or weakness.
  • You are recovering from surgery and your surgeon has not cleared you for this kind of exercise.
  • You have chest pain, shortness of breath or a sudden severe headache: call 911.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the full program?

Week 1 is on this page. All 8 weeks, with the videos for every exercise and progress tracking, are in the MD Fit app with a membership. Patients at the Sarasota clinic may be assigned it as part of their plan.

Can I do it without seeing Dr. Jared first?

Many people do, especially with the remote 1-on-1 options in the app. If you have pain you can't explain, recent surgery, or symptoms in the red-flag list, get evaluated first.

What if an exercise hurts?

Stop it. Every MD Fit program works inside a pain-free range; discomfort from effort is fine, sharp or worsening pain is not. Go back to the previous week's version or contact Dr. Jared.
