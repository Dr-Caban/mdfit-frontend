Arm, Elbow & Wrist Exercises

These are the 7 arm, elbow & wrist exercises in Dr. Jared Caban's video library, the same ones he assigns as homework at MD Fit in Sarasota. They work on the biceps, triceps and the forearm muscles behind tennis and golfer's elbow.

How to use this page

Exercises are grouped by type (strength, rehab and activation). Where Dr. Jared has written cues for an exercise in one of the muscle relief plans, they are shown here with the dose, and each cue names the muscle it is aimed at. 7 of the 7 exercises have written cues so far; the rest are listed with the muscles they are used for, and every one has a video in the free MD Fit app.

Pain usually comes from a muscle somewhere other than where it hurts, so the best exercise depends on the source. If you are not sure which muscle is involved, start from where it hurts or the muscle library, and follow the three-phase plan on that page: calm it down, restore movement, then strengthen.

Strength exercises

Bicep Curl (level 2 of 3, strength, at home or in a gym).

  • For the Brachioradialis (strengthen phase): Hold a dumbbell in each hand with your palms facing each other (thumb up). Bend your elbows to lift the weights toward your shoulders, keeping your wrists straight. Lower slowly. Dose: 3 x 10-12, every other day.
  • For the Supinator (strengthen phase): Perform standard bicep curls, focusing on the final part of the movement where you fully turn your palm up at the top. This strengthens the biceps, a primary supinator. Dose: 3 x 10-12, every other day.
  • For the Biceps, long head (strengthen phase): Lower over 3–4 seconds; keep the elbow at your side. Dose: 3 x 10 light, every other day.
  • For the Biceps, short head (strengthen phase): Slow 4-second lowering; increase weight only if pain-free next day. Dose: 3 x 10, every other day.

Tricep Extension (level 3 of 3, strength, at home or in a gym).

  • For the Triceps, long head (strengthen phase): Slow lowering, elbows steady. Dose: 3 x 12 light, every other day.
  • For the Triceps, lateral head (strengthen phase): Smooth, pain-free reps. Dose: 3 x 12, every other day.
  • For the Triceps, medial head (strengthen phase): Stop just short of hard lockout for the first two weeks. Dose: 3 x 12 light, every other day.

Tricep Kickback (level 4 of 3, strength, in a gym).

  • For the Triceps, lateral head (strengthen phase): Upper arm still, straighten with control, lower slowly. Dose: 3 x 12 light, every other day.

Overhead Tricep Extension (level 5 of 3, strength, at home or in a gym).

  • For the Triceps, long head (strengthen phase): Add only once the simpler extension is pain-free; this loads the long head at its longest. Dose: 3 x 8 light, twice weekly.

Rehab exercises

Eccentric Wrist Drops (level 2 of 3, rehab, at home or in a gym).

  • For the Brachioradialis (restore-movement phase): Rest your forearm on your thigh, palm down, holding a very light weight. Use your other hand to lift the weight up, then slowly lower it down over 3-5 seconds. Dose: 3 x 15, every other day.
  • For the Wrist extensors (strengthen phase): Lift with the other hand, lower slowly over 3–4 seconds. Mild discomfort is acceptable; sharp pain is not. Dose: 3 x 15, daily.
  • For the Finger extensors (strengthen phase): Slow lowering to build tendon tolerance. Dose: 3 x 15, daily.

Eccentric Wrist Pronator (level 2 of 3, rehab, at home or in a gym).

  • For the Supinator (restore-movement phase): Rest your forearm on your thigh, palm up, holding a light weight. Use your other hand to help turn your palm down, then slowly control the motion as the weight turns your palm back up. Dose: 3 x 15, every other day.
  • For the Biceps, long head (strengthen phase): Control turning the palm down to load the biceps gently. Dose: 3 x 12, every other day.
  • For the Biceps, short head (calm-it-down phase): Gentle, pain-free control to keep the area moving. Dose: 2 x 10 very light, daily.
  • For the Triceps, medial head (calm-it-down phase): Keep the forearm moving without load on the elbow. Dose: 2 x 10 very light, daily.

Also used in the relief plans for Wrist flexors, Finger flexors and Brachioradialis.

Activation exercises

Weighted Wrist Circles (level 2 of 3, activation, at home or in a gym).

  • For the Brachioradialis (calm-it-down phase): Without any weight, gently rotate your wrist in slow, controlled circles. Focus on a pain-free range of motion to improve joint mobility and blood flow. Dose: 2 x 10 circles each way, twice daily.
  • For the Triceps, lateral head (calm-it-down phase): Keep the forearm moving comfortably. Dose: 2 x 10 each way, no weight to start, daily.
  • For the Wrist extensors (calm-it-down phase): Gentle circles to keep the wrist moving. Dose: 2 x 10 each way, no weight to start, daily.
  • For the Finger extensors (calm-it-down phase): Keep the forearm and fingers moving. Dose: 2 x 10 each way, no weight, daily.

Also used in the relief plans for Wrist flexors, Finger flexors and Supinator.

Stop and get checked if

  • An exercise causes sharp, shooting or steadily worsening pain, or pain that lasts into the next day.
  • You have numbness, tingling or weakness that is new or spreading.
  • Pain follows a fall, an accident or a blow.
  • You have chest pain, shortness of breath or sudden severe headache: call 911.
