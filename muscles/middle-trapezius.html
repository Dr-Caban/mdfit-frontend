Where the middle trapezius sends pain

Burning ache between the shoulder blade and the spine, and the top of the shoulder. People usually come in describing pain at the on the shoulder blade, between the shoulder blades, top of the shoulder or inner edge of the shoulder blade.

What the middle trapezius does

Pulls the shoulder blades together and holds them back against the ribs.

  • Starts at: Spinous processes of the upper thoracic vertebrae (about T1 to T5).
  • Attaches to: Inner edge of the acromion and the spine of the shoulder blade.
  • Nerve supply: Spinal accessory nerve (cranial nerve XI) with sensory fibers from C3, C4.

Symptoms people describe

  • Burning or aching between the shoulder blade and the spine.
  • Ache over the top of the shoulder blade.
  • Worse after long desk sessions with rounded shoulders.
  • Feels like the area 'needs cracking'.

What makes it worse

Desk, phone or screens · Gripping, lifting or typing. If two or more of those match your day, this muscle moves up the list.

Quick self-check

Education, not a diagnosis. The more of these that ring true, the more likely the middle trapezius is involved:

  • Is the pain a burning ache between the spine and the inner edge of the shoulder blade?
  • Does it build up the longer you sit at a desk or on your phone?
  • Does squeezing the shoulder blades together feel weak or bring it on?
  • Do your shoulders round forward when you are tired?
  • Does it feel better when you move rather than rest?

Prone T hold. Lie face down with arms out to the sides, thumbs up, and lift the arms a few inches for 15 seconds. Suggests this muscle if: Burning or your familiar ache between the shoulder blades, or the arms feel weak and shaky.

What it gets mistaken for

  • Rhomboid trigger points
  • Thoracic facet joint irritation
  • Scalene referral
  • Rib dysfunction

Common causes

  • Rounded shoulders with tight chest muscles stretching the middle trap.
  • Long desk and phone sessions.
  • Carrying a heavy backpack.
  • Weakness from little pulling exercise.

Dr. Jared's 3-phase relief plan

Work through the phases in order and move on when the current one feels easy and pain-free. Every video is in the free MD Fit app.

Phase 1 · Calm it down

  1. Wall ball release · 60–90 sec, twice daily
    Place a ball between the spine and the shoulder blade against a wall and roll slowly, pausing on tender spots.
  2. Thoracic Rotation Seated (video) · 2 x 8 per side, daily
    Rotate gently through the upper back.

Phase 2 · Restore movement

  1. Baseball Cable Stretch (video) · 2 x 30 sec per side, daily
    Open the chest so the middle trap is not constantly stretched.
  2. Thoracic Extension Over Chair (video) · 2 x 8, daily
    Extend over the chair back to undo rounding.

Phase 3 · Strengthen

  1. Floor Slide Scapular Retract (video) · 3 x 10, daily
    Squeeze the shoulder blades back and slightly down as the arms slide.
  2. Face Pull (video) · 3 x 12, every other day
    Pull apart at face height with a pause.
  3. Dumbbell Row (video) · 3 x 10, twice weekly
    Start each rep by drawing the shoulder blade back.

Suggested library videos for this muscle: Floor Slide Scapular Retract, Face Pull, Thoracic Extension Over Chair.

Habits that help

  • Raise screens to eye level.
  • Set a reminder every 30 minutes to reset posture.
  • Use both backpack straps.

What to expect

Burning between the shoulder blades often eases in 1 to 2 weeks; endurance builds over 4 to 6 weeks.

When to see a professional

Book an evaluation if pain persists past 3 weeks, wakes you at night, or comes with chest symptoms (seek urgent care first). Get urgent care for any of these:

  • Pain between the shoulder blades with chest pain or shortness of breath: call 911.
  • Sudden tearing upper-back pain.
  • Pain with fever or unexplained weight loss.

Frequently asked questions

Can a tight middle trapezius cause pain at the on the shoulder blade?

Often, yes. Burning ache between the shoulder blade and the spine, and the top of the shoulder. That referred pain is why it gets blamed on the joint or the spot that hurts instead of the muscle.

How long does middle trapezius pain take to settle?

Burning between the shoulder blades often eases in 1 to 2 weeks; endurance builds over 4 to 6 weeks.

When should I see someone about it?

Book an evaluation if pain persists past 3 weeks, wakes you at night, or comes with chest symptoms (seek urgent care first).

What else can feel like middle trapezius pain?

Rhomboid trigger points, Thoracic facet joint irritation, Scalene referral. An evaluation separates muscle pain from these, and anything that needs a physician is referred on.
