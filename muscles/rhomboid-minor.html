Where the rhomboid minor sends pain

Along the inner edge of the upper shoulder blade, next to the spine. People usually come in describing pain at the inner edge of the shoulder blade or between the shoulder blades.

What the rhomboid minor does

Pulls the upper part of the shoulder blade toward the spine and slightly up.

  • Starts at: Spinous processes of C7 and T1.
  • Attaches to: Inner edge of the shoulder blade at the level of its spine.
  • Nerve supply: Dorsal scapular nerve (C4, C5).

Symptoms people describe

  • Ache along the inner edge of the upper shoulder blade, next to the spine.
  • A 'knot' high between the shoulder blades near the base of the neck.
  • Ache with long desk work or carrying a bag on one shoulder.
  • Feels better briefly after cracking or stretching.

What makes it worse

Desk, phone or screens · Gripping, lifting or typing. If two or more of those match your day, this muscle moves up the list.

Quick self-check

Education, not a diagnosis. The more of these that ring true, the more likely the rhomboid minor is involved:

  • Is the pain high, at the level of the base of the neck, right beside the spine?
  • Is there a knot along the inner edge of the top of the shoulder blade?
  • Do you carry a bag on one shoulder or sit for hours?
  • Does pulling the shoulder blades together reproduce it?
  • Does it ease when you move around?

Upper squeeze test. Pull the shoulder blades together and slightly up and hold for 10 seconds. Suggests this muscle if: Ache high between the shoulder blades near the spine.

What it gets mistaken for

  • Levator scapulae trigger points
  • Scalene referral
  • Cervical radiculopathy (C5)
  • Thoracic facet irritation

Common causes

  • Rounded shoulders stretching the rhomboids all day.
  • Carrying bags on one shoulder.
  • Long periods reaching forward (keyboard, steering).
  • Scalene or neck tension referring into the area.

Habits that help

  • Switch bag sides or use a backpack.
  • Keep the keyboard close so you are not reaching forward.

What to expect

Usually eases in 1 to 2 weeks.

When to see a professional

Book an evaluation if the knot keeps returning after 3 weeks or there are arm symptoms. Get urgent care for any of these:

  • Upper-back pain with chest pain or shortness of breath: call 911.
  • Arm weakness or numbness.
  • Night pain that does not change with position.

Frequently asked questions

Can a tight rhomboid minor cause pain at the inner edge of the shoulder blade?

Often, yes. Along the inner edge of the upper shoulder blade, next to the spine. That referred pain is why it gets blamed on the joint or the spot that hurts instead of the muscle.

How long does rhomboid minor pain take to settle?

Usually eases in 1 to 2 weeks.

When should I see someone about it?

Book an evaluation if the knot keeps returning after 3 weeks or there are arm symptoms.

What else can feel like rhomboid minor pain?

Levator scapulae trigger points, Scalene referral, Cervical radiculopathy (C5). An evaluation separates muscle pain from these, and anything that needs a physician is referred on.
