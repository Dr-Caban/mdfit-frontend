Where the triceps long head sends pain

Back of the shoulder and arm, up toward the base of the neck, and down the back of the forearm. People usually come in describing pain at the back of the upper arm, point of the elbow, forearm or back of the shoulder.

What the triceps long head does

Straightens the elbow and pulls the arm back and down; the only triceps head that crosses the shoulder.

  • Starts at: Just below the shoulder socket (infraglenoid tubercle of the shoulder blade).
  • Attaches to: Olecranon (the point of the elbow).
  • Nerve supply: Radial nerve (C6–C8).

Symptoms people describe

  • Ache at the back of the shoulder and the back of the upper arm.
  • Pain sometimes spreading up toward the top of the shoulder and base of the neck.
  • Ache down the back of the forearm.
  • Pain straightening the arm overhead, such as reaching up or pressing overhead.

What makes it worse

Reaching overhead · Gripping, lifting or typing. If two or more of those match your day, this muscle moves up the list.

Quick self-check

Education, not a diagnosis. The more of these that ring true, the more likely the triceps long head is involved:

  • Does the pain run along the back of the upper arm from the armpit toward the elbow?
  • Does reaching overhead with the elbow bent (like scratching your upper back) hurt?
  • Does straightening your arm against resistance bring it on?
  • Did it start after push-ups, dips, overhead pressing or tennis?
  • Does it also ache toward the top of your shoulder or neck?

Overhead elbow bend. Raise the arm overhead and bend the elbow so the hand drops behind your head. Gently push the elbow back with the other hand. Suggests this muscle if: Pulling or your familiar ache along the back of the upper arm and shoulder.

What it gets mistaken for

  • Teres major or minor trigger points
  • Posterior shoulder impingement
  • Cervical radiculopathy (C7)
  • Thoracic outlet syndrome

Common causes

  • Dips, push-ups, skull-crushers and overhead pressing.
  • Repetitive hammering or racquet sports.
  • Using crutches or a walker.
  • Long periods leaning on the elbows.

Dr. Jared's 3-phase relief plan

Work through the phases in order and move on when the current one feels easy and pain-free. Every video is in the free MD Fit app.

Phase 1 · Calm it down

  1. Triceps squeeze release · 60 sec, twice daily
    Grip the back of the upper arm near the armpit with the other hand and hold gentle pressure on tender spots while slowly bending and straightening the elbow.
  2. Thread The Needle (video) · 2 x 8 per side, daily
    Rotate gently to ease the back of the shoulder.

Phase 2 · Restore movement

  1. Overhead triceps stretch · 2 x 30 sec, daily
    Arm overhead, elbow bent, use the other hand to ease the elbow back. Keep the ribs down.
  2. Thoracic Extension Over Chair (video) · 2 x 8, daily
    Improve upper-back extension so overhead reaching is easier.

Phase 3 · Strengthen

  1. Tricep Extension (video) · 3 x 12 light, every other day
    Slow lowering, elbows steady.
  2. Overhead Tricep Extension (video) · 3 x 8 light, twice weekly
    Add only once the simpler extension is pain-free; this loads the long head at its longest.

Suggested library videos for this muscle: Tricep Extension, Thread The Needle, Overhead Tricep Extension.

Habits that help

  • Avoid leaning on the elbows for long periods.
  • Build dips and pressing volume gradually.

What to expect

Usually improves within 2 to 3 weeks; overhead pressing tolerance over 4 to 6 weeks.

When to see a professional

Book an evaluation if hand numbness, wrist weakness or elbow swelling appear, or pain persists past 3 weeks. Get urgent care for any of these:

  • A pop at the back of the elbow with weakness straightening the arm.
  • Numbness on the back of the hand with wrist drop (radial nerve).
  • Swelling at the point of the elbow with redness or fever.

Frequently asked questions

Can a tight triceps long head cause pain at the back of the upper arm?

Often, yes. Back of the shoulder and arm, up toward the base of the neck, and down the back of the forearm. That referred pain is why it gets blamed on the joint or the spot that hurts instead of the muscle.

How long does triceps long head pain take to settle?

Usually improves within 2 to 3 weeks; overhead pressing tolerance over 4 to 6 weeks.

When should I see someone about it?

Book an evaluation if hand numbness, wrist weakness or elbow swelling appear, or pain persists past 3 weeks.

What else can feel like triceps long head pain?

Teres major or minor trigger points, Posterior shoulder impingement, Cervical radiculopathy (C7). An evaluation separates muscle pain from these, and anything that needs a physician is referred on.
