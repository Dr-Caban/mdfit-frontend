Where the triceps medial head sends pain

The point of the elbow and the inner elbow, a golfer's-elbow look-alike, sometimes the ring and little fingers. People usually come in describing pain at the point of the elbow or ring and little finger. It is also a common hidden driver behind Golfer's Elbow (Medial Epicondylitis).

What the triceps medial head does

Straightens the elbow in every push and keeps the joint capsule from pinching.

  • Starts at: Back of the upper arm bone, below the radial groove (deep to the other two heads).
  • Attaches to: Olecranon and the back of the elbow joint capsule.
  • Nerve supply: Radial nerve (C6–C8), with a branch shared near the ulnar nerve.

Symptoms people describe

  • Pain at the point of the elbow.
  • Ache at the inner elbow that can mimic golfer's elbow.
  • Sometimes ache into the ring and little fingers.
  • Pain at the end of straightening the elbow or leaning on it.

What makes it worse

Gripping, lifting or typing. If two or more of those match your day, this muscle moves up the list.

Quick self-check

Education, not a diagnosis. The more of these that ring true, the more likely the triceps medial head is involved:

  • Is the pain right at the point of the elbow or just above it?
  • Does fully straightening the elbow against resistance hurt?
  • Does leaning on the elbow aggravate it?
  • Do you also feel ache on the inner elbow?
  • Does it sometimes ache toward the ring and little fingers?

End-range extension. Straighten the elbow fully and press the hand down against a table for 5 seconds. Suggests this muscle if: Ache at or just above the point of the elbow.

What it gets mistaken for

  • Medial epicondylitis (golfer's elbow)
  • Olecranon bursitis
  • Ulnar nerve irritation (cubital tunnel)
  • Triceps tendinopathy

Common causes

  • Lockout in presses and push-ups.
  • Leaning on elbows at a desk.
  • Throwing and racquet sports.
  • Using crutches.

Dr. Jared's 3-phase relief plan

Work through the phases in order and move on when the current one feels easy and pain-free. Every video is in the free MD Fit app.

Phase 1 · Calm it down

  1. Above-elbow pressure release · 60 sec, twice daily
    Press gently on both sides just above the point of the elbow and slowly bend and straighten.
  2. Eccentric Wrist Pronator (video) · 2 x 10 very light, daily
    Keep the forearm moving without load on the elbow.

Phase 2 · Restore movement

  1. Gentle elbow bends · 2 x 15, twice daily
    Slowly bend and straighten the elbow through a comfortable range, no weight.

Phase 3 · Strengthen

  1. Tricep Extension (video) · 3 x 12 light, every other day
    Stop just short of hard lockout for the first two weeks.
  2. Wall Push Up (video) · 3 x 12, every other day
    Smooth pushing without slamming into lockout.

Suggested library videos for this muscle: Tricep Extension, Eccentric Wrist Pronator.

Habits that help

  • Pad or avoid leaning on the elbows.
  • Avoid snapping into lockout during presses.

What to expect

Usually settles in 2 to 3 weeks.

When to see a professional

Book an evaluation if there is swelling at the elbow tip, finger numbness or no improvement in 3 weeks. Get urgent care for any of these:

  • A swollen, red, hot bump at the point of the elbow (infected bursa).
  • Numbness and weakness in the ring and little fingers.
  • Inability to straighten the elbow after a fall.

How long does triceps medial head pain take to settle?

Usually settles in 2 to 3 weeks.

When should I see someone about it?

Book an evaluation if there is swelling at the elbow tip, finger numbness or no improvement in 3 weeks.

What else can feel like triceps medial head pain?

Medial epicondylitis (golfer's elbow), Olecranon bursitis, Ulnar nerve irritation (cubital tunnel). An evaluation separates muscle pain from these, and anything that needs a physician is referred on.
