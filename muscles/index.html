The Muscle Library

★★★★★ 5.0 · 89 Google reviews

Pain is usually felt somewhere other than the muscle causing it. These pages explain, muscle by muscle, where each one sends pain, how to check it and what to do, drawn from Dr. Jared Caban's referred-pain work in the clinic.

Start from where it hurts with the pain-location pages, or pick a muscle below. Each page has symptoms, a self-check, look-alikes, red flags and a three-phase relief plan with free videos.

Ankle & foot

  • Tibialis anteriorFront of the shin, front of the ankle and the big toe.
  • PeronealsOuter ankle and outside of the foot; common after ankle sprains.

Back of head

  • OccipitalisAche at the back of the head that can shoot through the skull to behind the eye, with scalp tenderness.
  • Splenius capitis & cervicisPain on the top of the head, and deep ache inside the head behind the eye.

Back of thigh

  • Semitendinosus & semimembranosusThe buttock crease, back of the thigh and the inner back of the knee, sometimes the inner calf.

Calf

  • PopliteusBack of the knee; worse crouching, walking downhill or running.
  • GastrocnemiusUpper calf, back of the knee and the arch of the foot; deep cramp-like tightness.

Chest

  • Anterior deltoidFront of the shoulder and down the front of the upper arm, close to the muscle itself.
  • Pectoralis major upper headFront of the shoulder over the front delt, often mistaken for a front-shoulder joint problem.
  • Pectoralis major lower headFront of the chest, down the inner arm to the inner elbow and the ring and little fingers.
  • SubclaviusUnder the collarbone and down the front of the arm and forearm into the thumb, index and middle fingers.
  • ScalenesChest, front and back of the upper arm, inner shoulder blade and down the forearm to the thumb and index finge
  • Pectoralis minorFront of the shoulder and chest, down the inner arm to the last three fingers.
  • IntercostalsSharp or aching pain along the rib, worse with deep breaths, coughing or twisting.

Elbow & forearm

  • Biceps short headFront of the shoulder near the collarbone tip, the inner front of the arm and the elbow crease.

Foot

  • SoleusHeel and Achilles tendon, lower calf; can contribute to plantar fasciitis.
  • Tibialis posteriorInner ankle and arch, along the inner shin; common with flat feet.
  • Foot musclesHeel and sole of the foot; first-step-in-the-morning pain like plantar fasciitis.

Forearm

  • Wrist extensorsOutside of the elbow (tennis elbow) and the back of the wrist and hand on the thumb side.
  • Finger extensorsBack of the forearm and back of the hand into the middle or ring finger; a 'stiff fingers' feeling.
  • Wrist flexorsInside of the elbow (golfer's elbow) and the palm side of the wrist.
  • Finger flexorsPalm and the front of the fingers, sometimes a 'trigger finger' catching feeling.
  • BrachioradialisOutside of the elbow and the web of the thumb.
  • SupinatorOuter elbow and the thumb web; common in tennis elbow that won't settle.

Glutes

  • Biceps femorisBack of the knee on the outside and up the back of the thigh.
  • Vastus lateralisDown the outer thigh to the outside of the knee; can make lying on that side painful.

Head & face

  • TemporalisTemple headache, above the eyebrow and into the upper teeth.
  • MasseterJaw, cheek, in front of the ear and the back teeth.

Hip

  • Gluteus minimusOuter hip down the side of the thigh and leg to the ankle, or down the back of the thigh and calf.
  • PiriformisMid-buttock and back of the hip, down the back-outside of the thigh toward the calf.
  • Gluteus maximusButtock and the tailbone; aches with long sitting and walking uphill.

Hip & groin

  • External obliqueAbdominal wall, the side of the belly and down into the groin; can feel like heartburn or a side stitch.
  • Adductor longus & brevisDeep groin, the inner thigh and down to the inner knee and top of the shin.
  • Adductor magnusDeep inside the pelvis and groin, and the inner thigh from the groin toward the knee.
  • PsoasVertical band along the low back, plus the front of the thigh and groin.
  • IliacusGroin, front of the thigh, and the back of the pelvis high in the glute.
  • Tensor fasciae lataeFront-outer hip down the outside of the thigh, usually stopping at the knee.
  • Rectus femorisA deep ache around and behind the kneecap, and the front of the hip bone.

Knee

  • Vastus intermediusFront of the mid-thigh, worse climbing stairs or standing up from a chair.
  • Vastus medialisInner knee and inside of the kneecap; can make the knee feel like it buckles.

Low back

  • IliocostalisLow back and buttock from the lumbar part; the lower shoulder blade and even the belly from the thoracic part.
  • Quadratus lumborumLow back and flank, over the SI joint and buttock, and the point of the hip.
  • Gluteus mediusAlong the belt line, over the SI joint, the buttock and the top of the hip.

Low back & hip

  • LongissimusLow in the buttock and over the SI joint, often well below where the muscle actually is.
  • MultifidusDeep, pinpoint pain right next to the spine, the sacrum and sometimes the tailbone.

Neck & shoulder

  • Lateral deltoidOutside of the shoulder cap and the side of the upper arm toward the deltoid's insertion.
  • Posterior deltoidBack of the shoulder and the back of the upper arm.
  • Middle trapeziusBurning ache between the shoulder blade and the spine, and the top of the shoulder.
  • Rhomboid minorAlong the inner edge of the upper shoulder blade, next to the spine.
  • SuboccipitalsA band of headache from the base of the skull wrapping to behind the eye and the forehead.
  • Upper trapeziusUp the side of the neck, behind the ear and into the temple (a question-mark shape).
  • SternocleidomastoidForehead, around and behind the eye, cheek, ear and top of the head.
  • Levator scapulaeThe angle where the neck meets the shoulder, down the inner edge of the shoulder blade.

Ribs

  • Serratus anteriorSide of the rib cage and the bottom tip of the shoulder blade; a side "stitch" that hurts with deep breaths.
  • Latissimus dorsiBottom tip of the shoulder blade and down the back of the arm to the ring and little fingers.

Shoulder

  • Teres majorBack of the shoulder and the back of the upper arm, sometimes the back of the forearm.
  • Biceps long headFront of the shoulder over the biceps groove and down the front of the arm toward the elbow crease.
  • SupraspinatusDeep ache at the top and side of the shoulder that can run down the outside of the arm to the elbow.
  • InfraspinatusDeep in the front of the shoulder joint, down the front and outside of the arm.
  • Teres minorA localized deep ache at the back of the shoulder.
  • SubscapularisBack of the shoulder, down the back of the arm, sometimes a band around the wrist.

Shoulder (front)

  • CoracobrachialisFront of the shoulder, the back of the upper arm and forearm, and the back of the hand toward the middle finge

Upper arm

  • Triceps long headBack of the shoulder and arm, up toward the base of the neck, and down the back of the forearm.
  • Triceps lateral headBack of the upper arm and the outside of the elbow, a common tennis-elbow look-alike, and the back of the fore
  • Triceps medial headThe point of the elbow and the inner elbow, a golfer's-elbow look-alike, sometimes the ring and little fingers

Upper back

  • Lower trapeziusUp the back of the neck to the base of the skull, the top of the shoulder, and along the inner shoulder blade.
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blade and the spine.
