Where the biceps long head sends pain

Front of the shoulder over the biceps groove and down the front of the arm toward the elbow crease. People usually come in describing pain at the front of the elbow, front of the shoulder or deep inside the shoulder joint.

What the biceps long head does

Bends the elbow, turns the palm up and helps hold the ball of the shoulder down in the socket.

  • Starts at: Top of the shoulder socket (supraglenoid tubercle), running through the groove at the front of the upper arm.
  • Attaches to: Radial tuberosity of the forearm, below the elbow.
  • Nerve supply: Musculocutaneous nerve (C5, C6).

Symptoms people describe

  • Ache at the front of the shoulder over the biceps groove.
  • Pain down the front of the arm toward the elbow crease.
  • Pain lifting with the palm up, or reaching overhead.
  • A tender cord you can roll under your fingers at the front of the shoulder.

What makes it worse

Reaching overhead · Gripping, lifting or typing. If two or more of those match your day, this muscle moves up the list.

Quick self-check

Education, not a diagnosis. The more of these that ring true, the more likely the biceps long head is involved:

  • Is there a tender spot in the groove at the front of the shoulder that moves when you rotate the arm?
  • Does lifting or carrying with the palm up hurt at the front of the shoulder?
  • Does the pain run down the front of the upper arm?
  • Does reaching overhead or throwing bring it on?
  • Do you hear a click or snap at the front of the shoulder?

Speed's style palm-up raise. Hold the arm straight in front, palm up, and raise it against your other hand's resistance. Suggests this muscle if: Pain at the front of the shoulder over the biceps groove.

What it gets mistaken for

  • Biceps tendinopathy or SLAP tear
  • Infraspinatus trigger points
  • Anterior deltoid strain
  • Shoulder impingement

Common causes

  • Repeated overhead work and throwing.
  • Heavy curls and carrying with the palms up.
  • Rotator cuff weakness so the biceps works as a stabilizer.
  • Rounded shoulders that pinch the tendon.

Dr. Jared's 3-phase relief plan

Work through the phases in order and move on when the current one feels easy and pain-free. Every video is in the free MD Fit app.

Phase 1 · Calm it down

  1. Biceps groove release · 60 sec, twice daily
    Use gentle fingertip pressure on the biceps muscle belly (not on the tendon itself) and slowly bend and straighten the elbow.
  2. Shoulder Drop Hold (video) · 3 x 20 sec, daily
    Settle the shoulder down and back to unload the tendon.

Phase 2 · Restore movement

  1. Baseball Cable Stretch (video) · 2 x 30 sec per side, daily
    Arm straight, thumb pointing down and slightly behind you to lengthen the long head.
  2. Rotator Cuff External Rotation (video) · 2 x 15, daily
    Strengthen the cuff so the biceps does not have to stabilize.

Phase 3 · Strengthen

  1. Bicep Curl (video) · 3 x 10 light, every other day
    Lower over 3–4 seconds; keep the elbow at your side.
  2. Eccentric Wrist Pronator (video) · 3 x 12, every other day
    Control turning the palm down to load the biceps gently.

Suggested library videos for this muscle: Baseball Cable Stretch, Eccentric Wrist Pronator, Bicep Curl.

Habits that help

  • Carry with palms facing your body rather than up.
  • Limit long overhead work.
  • Keep shoulders back when working at a desk.

What to expect

Tendon-region pain usually eases over 2 to 4 weeks; full overhead and lifting tolerance in 6 to 8 weeks.

When to see a professional

Book an evaluation if there is clicking or catching, a bulge in the arm, or no improvement in 3 weeks. Get urgent care for any of these:

  • A pop followed by a 'Popeye' bulge in the upper arm (tendon rupture).
  • Shoulder that catches, locks or feels unstable.
  • Bruising down the arm after lifting.

Frequently asked questions

Can a tight biceps long head cause pain at the front of the elbow?

Often, yes. Front of the shoulder over the biceps groove and down the front of the arm toward the elbow crease. That referred pain is why it gets blamed on the joint or the spot that hurts instead of the muscle.

How long does biceps long head pain take to settle?

Tendon-region pain usually eases over 2 to 4 weeks; full overhead and lifting tolerance in 6 to 8 weeks.

When should I see someone about it?

Book an evaluation if there is clicking or catching, a bulge in the arm, or no improvement in 3 weeks.

What else can feel like biceps long head pain?

Biceps tendinopathy or SLAP tear, Infraspinatus trigger points, Anterior deltoid strain. An evaluation separates muscle pain from these, and anything that needs a physician is referred on.
