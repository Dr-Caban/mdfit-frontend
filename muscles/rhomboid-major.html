Where the rhomboid major sends pain

Between the shoulder blade and the spine. Often strained because tight chest muscles pull the blades apart. People usually come in describing pain at the between the shoulder blades.

What the rhomboid major does

Pulls the shoulder blade toward the spine and down; steadies it when you pull.

  • Starts at: Spinous processes of T2 to T5.
  • Attaches to: Inner edge of the shoulder blade below its spine, down to the bottom corner.
  • Nerve supply: Dorsal scapular nerve (C4, C5).

Symptoms people describe

  • Ache between the shoulder blade and the spine, lower down.
  • A superficial nagging ache that is hard to reach.
  • Worse after desk work, driving or rowing.
  • Often appears alongside tight chest muscles.

What makes it worse

Desk, phone or screens · Gripping, lifting or typing. If two or more of those match your day, this muscle moves up the list.

Quick self-check

Education, not a diagnosis. The more of these that ring true, the more likely the rhomboid major is involved:

  • Is the ache between the spine and the lower half of the shoulder blade?
  • Is it a nagging, superficial ache rather than sharp?
  • Are your chest muscles tight and shoulders rounded?
  • Does it build up with desk work or driving?
  • Does pressing on the area reproduce it?

Shoulder blade squeeze. Squeeze the shoulder blades together and down and hold for 10 seconds. Suggests this muscle if: Ache between the lower shoulder blade and the spine.

What it gets mistaken for

  • Middle trapezius trigger points
  • Scalene referral
  • Rib or thoracic facet irritation
  • Gallbladder pain on the right side (rare)

Common causes

  • Tight chest muscles pulling the shoulder blades apart.
  • Long sitting with the arms forward.
  • Heavy rowing without good form.
  • Weak middle and lower traps.

Dr. Jared's 3-phase relief plan

Work through the phases in order and move on when the current one feels easy and pain-free. Every video is in the free MD Fit app.

Phase 1 · Calm it down

  1. Rhomboid ball release · 60–90 sec, twice daily
    Lie on a ball placed between the spine and the lower shoulder blade, arms crossed to spread the blades.
  2. Thread The Needle (video) · 2 x 8 per side, daily
    Rotate to ease the area.

Phase 2 · Restore movement

  1. Baseball Cable Stretch (video) · 2 x 30 sec per side, daily
    Release the chest that pulls the shoulder blades apart.
  2. Thoracic Extension Over Chair (video) · 2 x 8, daily
    Open up the upper back.

Phase 3 · Strengthen

  1. Floor Slide Scapular Retract (video) · 3 x 10, daily
    Squeeze back and down with control.
  2. Dumbbell Row (video) · 3 x 10, twice weekly
    Pause at the top with the shoulder blade drawn in.

Suggested library videos for this muscle: Floor Slide Scapular Retract, Thread The Needle, Thoracic Extension Over Chair.

Habits that help

  • Stretch the chest daily, not just the upper back.
  • Take posture breaks every 30 minutes.

What to expect

Usually eases within 1 to 3 weeks once the chest is released and the back strengthened.

When to see a professional

Book an evaluation if pain persists past 3 weeks or keeps returning. Get urgent care for any of these:

  • Pain with chest symptoms or shortness of breath: call 911.
  • Right-sided pain after fatty meals with nausea.
  • Pain with fever or weight loss.

Can a tight rhomboid major cause pain at the between the shoulder blades?

Often, yes. Between the shoulder blade and the spine. Often strained because tight chest muscles pull the blades apart. That referred pain is why it gets blamed on the joint or the spot that hurts instead of the muscle.

How long does rhomboid major pain take to settle?

Usually eases within 1 to 3 weeks once the chest is released and the back strengthened.

When should I see someone about it?

Book an evaluation if pain persists past 3 weeks or keeps returning.

What else can feel like rhomboid major pain?

Middle trapezius trigger points, Scalene referral, Rib or thoracic facet irritation. An evaluation separates muscle pain from these, and anything that needs a physician is referred on.
