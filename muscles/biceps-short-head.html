Where the biceps short head sends pain

Front of the shoulder near the collarbone tip, the inner front of the arm and the elbow crease. People usually come in describing pain at the front of the elbow or front of the shoulder.

What the biceps short head does

Bends the elbow, turns the palm up and pulls the arm forward and across the body.

  • Starts at: Tip of the coracoid process at the front of the shoulder blade.
  • Attaches to: Radial tuberosity below the elbow (shared tendon with the long head).
  • Nerve supply: Musculocutaneous nerve (C5, C6).

Symptoms people describe

  • Ache at the front of the shoulder, closer to the chest than the groove.
  • Pain on the inner front of the upper arm.
  • Pain at the elbow crease when lifting or curling.
  • Tenderness on the inner half of the biceps belly.

What makes it worse

Gripping, lifting or typing. If two or more of those match your day, this muscle moves up the list.

Quick self-check

Education, not a diagnosis. The more of these that ring true, the more likely the biceps short head is involved:

  • Is the tender spot on the inner half of the biceps, toward the body?
  • Does curling or carrying with the palm up hurt at the elbow crease?
  • Is the shoulder ache nearer the chest than the outside of the shoulder?
  • Did it start after curls, pull-ups or heavy carrying?
  • Does reaching across your body bring it on?

Resisted curl and turn. With the elbow bent at 90 degrees and the palm down, turn the palm up against resistance from your other hand. Suggests this muscle if: Ache in the inner biceps or elbow crease.

What it gets mistaken for

  • Distal biceps tendinopathy
  • Coracobrachialis trigger points
  • Brachialis strain
  • Pronator syndrome

Common causes

  • Heavy curls and chin-ups.
  • Carrying boxes with the palms up.
  • Climbing and gymnastics.
  • Sudden lifting with the elbow straight.

Dr. Jared's 3-phase relief plan

Work through the phases in order and move on when the current one feels easy and pain-free. Every video is in the free MD Fit app.

Phase 1 · Calm it down

  1. Inner biceps release · 60 sec, twice daily
    Gently squeeze the inner half of the biceps between thumb and fingers while slowly straightening the elbow.
  2. Eccentric Wrist Pronator (video) · 2 x 10 very light, daily
    Gentle, pain-free control to keep the area moving.

Phase 2 · Restore movement

  1. Baseball Cable Stretch (video) · 2 x 30 sec per side, daily
    Arm out to the side and slightly back, palm facing back.

Phase 3 · Strengthen

  1. Bicep Curl (video) · 3 x 10, every other day
    Slow 4-second lowering; increase weight only if pain-free next day.
  2. Row (video) · 3 x 10, twice weekly
    Smooth pulling to rebuild arm and back strength together.

Suggested library videos for this muscle: Baseball Cable Stretch, Bicep Curl, Eccentric Wrist Pronator.

Habits that help

  • Lift boxes with the elbows bent and close to the body.
  • Build curl and chin-up volume gradually.

What to expect

Most cases ease in 2 to 3 weeks; strength returns over 4 to 6 weeks.

When to see a professional

Book an evaluation urgently after a pop at the elbow; otherwise if pain persists beyond 3 weeks. Get urgent care for any of these:

  • A pop at the elbow with bruising and a lump moving up the arm (tendon rupture).
  • Inability to turn the palm up with strength.
  • Numbness in the forearm.

Frequently asked questions

Can a tight biceps short head cause pain at the front of the elbow?

Often, yes. Front of the shoulder near the collarbone tip, the inner front of the arm and the elbow crease. That referred pain is why it gets blamed on the joint or the spot that hurts instead of the muscle.

How long does biceps short head pain take to settle?

Most cases ease in 2 to 3 weeks; strength returns over 4 to 6 weeks.

When should I see someone about it?

Book an evaluation urgently after a pop at the elbow; otherwise if pain persists beyond 3 weeks.

What else can feel like biceps short head pain?

Distal biceps tendinopathy, Coracobrachialis trigger points, Brachialis strain. An evaluation separates muscle pain from these, and anything that needs a physician is referred on.
