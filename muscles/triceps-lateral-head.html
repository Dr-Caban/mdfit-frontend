Where the triceps lateral head sends pain

Back of the upper arm and the outside of the elbow, a common tennis-elbow look-alike, and the back of the forearm. People usually come in describing pain at the back of the upper arm. It is also a common hidden driver behind Tennis Elbow (Lateral Epicondylitis).

What the triceps lateral head does

Straightens the elbow with force; the outer 'horseshoe' on the back of the arm.

  • Starts at: Back of the upper arm bone, above the radial groove.
  • Attaches to: Olecranon (the point of the elbow).
  • Nerve supply: Radial nerve (C6–C8).

Symptoms people describe

  • Ache in the back of the upper arm on the outer side.
  • Pain at the outside of the elbow that can be mistaken for tennis elbow.
  • Ache down the back of the forearm.
  • Pain pushing up from a chair or doing push-ups.

What makes it worse

Gripping, lifting or typing. If two or more of those match your day, this muscle moves up the list.

Quick self-check

Education, not a diagnosis. The more of these that ring true, the more likely the triceps lateral head is involved:

  • Is the tender spot on the outer back of the upper arm, a hand-width above the elbow?
  • Do you have outer-elbow pain but gripping is not the main trigger?
  • Does pushing up from a chair or a push-up bring it on?
  • Do hammering or racquet sports aggravate it?
  • Does it ache down the back of the forearm?

Resisted elbow straightening. Bend the elbow to 90 degrees and push the hand down against your other hand, trying to straighten the arm. Suggests this muscle if: Ache in the outer back of the arm or the outside of the elbow.

What it gets mistaken for

  • Lateral epicondylitis (tennis elbow)
  • Radial tunnel syndrome
  • Supinator trigger points
  • Cervical radiculopathy (C7)

Common causes

  • Push-ups, dips and bench pressing.
  • Hammering and tool work.
  • Tennis backhand.
  • Pushing up from chairs with weak legs.

  1. Tricep Kickback (video) · 3 x 12 light, every other day

Habits that help

  • Use the legs, not the arms, to stand up from chairs.
  • Warm up the elbow before tennis or tool work.

What to expect

Usually eases in 2 to 3 weeks with load management and release.

When to see a professional

Book an evaluation if outer-elbow pain persists past 3 weeks or there is any hand weakness or numbness. Get urgent care for any of these:

  • Wrist drop or numbness on the back of the hand.
  • Elbow swelling with redness or fever.
  • A pop with weakness straightening the arm.

Frequently asked questions

Can a tight triceps lateral head cause pain at the back of the upper arm?

Often, yes. Back of the upper arm and the outside of the elbow, a common tennis-elbow look-alike, and the back of the forearm. That referred pain is why it gets blamed on the joint or the spot that hurts instead of the muscle.

How long does triceps lateral head pain take to settle?

Usually eases in 2 to 3 weeks with load management and release.

When should I see someone about it?

Book an evaluation if outer-elbow pain persists past 3 weeks or there is any hand weakness or numbness.

What else can feel like triceps lateral head pain?

Lateral epicondylitis (tennis elbow), Radial tunnel syndrome, Supinator trigger points. An evaluation separates muscle pain from these, and anything that needs a physician is referred on.
