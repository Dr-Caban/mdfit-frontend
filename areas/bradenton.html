How to get here

From downtown Bradenton or Riverwalk: about 25 minutes south on US-41. From West Bradenton and Palma Sola: about 25 minutes via US-41. From Lakewood Ranch and Lakewood Ranch Main Street: about 20 minutes south on I-75 to Clark Road. From Longboat Key: about 25 minutes via Longboat Pass and US-41.

There are two ways down from Manatee County. The interstate route is Manatee Avenue (SR-64) or 53rd Avenue (SR-70) east to I-75, south past University Parkway, Fruitville and Bee Ridge to Clark Road (exit 205), then west; it is faster from East Bradenton and near the river bridges. The surface route is US-41 the whole way, past the airport and downtown Sarasota to Clark Road: slower but predictable, and the natural choice from Cortez Road, Palma Sola and the Anna Maria Island side. The clinic has its own lot a short distance east of US-41.

Who we see from Bradenton

Golfers from IMG Academy, Lakewood Ranch and the Legacy. Pickleball players from the busy Bradenton courts. Office workers from downtown Bradenton with chronic neck and shoulder pain. Older adults rehabbing after knee and hip replacement at local hospitals. Weekend athletes recovering from running, cycling and racquet-sport injuries.

Bradenton skews younger and more year-round than the towns south of us: families with high-school and academy athletes, people with physical jobs or a long I-75 commute, boaters on the river and Palma Sola Bay, and a strong weightlifting and CrossFit crowd that wants to train around an injury rather than stop. Seasonal residents on the river and Anna Maria Island usually book a block of visits from January to April.

Problems that make the drive worth it

  • A shoulder that has stopped tolerating overhead work: rotator cuff tendon pain, bursitis, or a frozen shoulder that has not loosened on its own.
  • Back pain that keeps returning: disc-related pain, sciatica-type symptoms down the back of the thigh, or a guarded psoas from long hours in a truck or at a desk.
  • Knees in gym athletes and runners: patellar tendinopathy, pain around the kneecap, and meniscus recovery with or without surgery.
  • Boat and dock injuries: ankle sprains that never stabilized, chronic ankle instability, and outer-ankle pain from weak peroneals.
  • New parents and postpartum return to exercise, including pelvic floor and SI joint issues that linger after the first year.

What we treat

Back pain, sciatica, neck pain, rotator cuff and shoulder pain, knee pain, hip pain, chronic pain, post-surgical rehab, sports injury rehab, medical massage, and strength and performance training. See the Conditions page for the full list.

Why clients drive to Clark Road

Visits are longer than in most insurance-based clinics (typically 45–60 minutes 1-on-1 with Dr. Jared himself, every visit). Treatment is paired with a home-exercise program in the MD Fit app so your drive buys you a plan, not just a session. Many Bradenton clients come once a week and work independently in between.

Making one trip count

Because the round trip is close to an hour in the car, each Bradenton visit is built to do a lot. The evaluation is about 90 minutes and $300: history, movement screen, hands-on work and the first version of your plan in one appointment. Follow-ups are $150. MD Fit is a self-pay practice: we don't bill insurance, no referral is needed, and you pay at the visit. Early or late appointments help the drive miss the worst of University Parkway and the airport stretch of US-41. Once the plan is running, a video visit can stand in for some follow-ups, and a free phone consult is the no-cost way to find out, before you drive, whether the clinic fits your problem.

Between visits

The free MD Fit app is where the Bradenton part of the plan happens: your assigned videos with reps, tempo and stop rules written down, so a twelve-minute routine in your garage, at a Bradenton gym or on the dock does not depend on remembering what was said in Sarasota. Dr. Jared updates the list after each visit, which is what lets in-person visits stretch from weekly to every two or three weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth the drive from Bradenton?

Clients tell us yes, because every session is 1-on-1 with Dr. Jared and progress is faster when care is consistent.

Do you have appointments after work?

The clinic is open Mon–Fri 8 AM to 4 PM. Evening appointments are not available.

Can I combine a session with a visit to Siesta Key or St. Armands?

Many clients do exactly that. We can time your appointment to fit a weekend in Sarasota.

Do you take insurance?

No. MD Fit is self-pay, which is what makes one clinician for the whole visit, long appointments and no visit caps possible. Some clients submit the receipt to their plan or HSA/FSA themselves; check with your plan.

Which route is faster from Bradenton, I-75 or US-41?

From East Bradenton and Lakewood Ranch, I-75 to exit 205 is usually quicker. From West Bradenton, Cortez and the islands, US-41 is simpler and about the same time.

Can some follow-ups be done by video?

Often, once the plan is established. Dr. Jared reviews your movement on camera and adjusts the program in the app. Hands-on treatment still needs an in-person visit.
