How to get here

From downtown Osprey: about 10 minutes north on US-41. From Oaks Preserve and The Oaks Clubside: 10–15 minutes. From Nokomis and Casey Key: about 15 minutes. Easy parking on-site.

There is only one road to think about. Head north on US-41 past Oscar Scherer State Park and Historic Spanish Point, continue through the Palmer Ranch stretch, and turn right onto Clark Road. The clinic is just a short distance in, on the north side, with its own lot. From Casey Key, take Blackburn Point Road back to US-41 first. From the eastern edge of Palmer Ranch, Honore Avenue north to Clark Road is an easy alternative that skips the Tamiami Trail lights altogether. Nobody from Osprey needs the interstate.

Who we see from Osprey and Nokomis

Golfers from The Oaks and nearby clubs. Boaters and anglers with low back and shoulder pain. Retirees managing chronic pain and recovery after joint replacement. Pickleball players from the local courts. People who want to keep hiking, cycling and swimming into their 70s and beyond.

On the water

Osprey and Nokomis live on Little Sarasota Bay and the Intracoastal, and the water produces its own injuries. Hauling a cooler onto a boat, cranking a winch, casting for hours and climbing in and out of a kayak all load the same places: the lats and infraspinatus in the shoulder, the quadratus lumborum on the side you always lift from, and the forearm. We see shoulder impingement, tennis elbow from rod and line work, low back pain that flares after a day offshore, and flank or rib-side pain from twisting under load on a moving deck. The fix is usually a mix of hands-on release and strength in the positions the boat demands.

On the Legacy Trail

The Legacy Trail runs right through Osprey, and a lot of our neighbors are on it daily. Cyclists come in with neck and wrist pain from long hours in the drops and with kneecap pain from a saddle that is a little too low. Walkers and runners bring plantar fasciitis, Achilles and soleus tightness, and IT band pain on the outside of the knee. Because the trail is flat, pavement and repetition rather than hills drive most of these, which means the solution lives in strength and tissue capacity more than in a new pair of shoes.

Staying active for the long haul

Many Osprey clients are in their sixties, seventies and eighties and are not trying to get back to a sport so much as to keep the ones they have. Strength and balance work for older adults is a large part of what we do here: knee and hip osteoarthritis that is being managed rather than operated on, spinal stenosis that limits how far you can walk the beach at Nokomis, and balance training after a near-miss on a dock or a wet pool deck. Swimmers at the community pools are common, and swimming is often the activity we protect first while the rest is rebuilt.

What we treat

Back pain, sciatica, neck pain, rotator cuff and shoulder pain, knee and hip pain, chronic pain, post-surgical rehab, sports injury rehab, medical massage, functional mobility, balance training, and strength and performance training. See Conditions for the full list.

Easy weekly follow-up

Because the drive is short, many Osprey clients come weekly for the first month, then taper to every other week. That consistency is often the difference between slow improvement and real change.

First visit, then what

The first appointment is an evaluation, about 90 minutes and $300, and it includes treatment, not just testing: Dr. Jared takes the history, assesses how you move, works on the tissue involved and sets up your home program before you leave. Follow-ups are $150 and run about an hour. MD Fit is a self-pay practice: we don't bill insurance, no referral is needed, and you pay at the visit; evaluation $300 for about 90 minutes, follow-ups $150. The home program lives in the free MD Fit app, with Dr. Jared's own videos and the exact reps and tempo, so the work between visits happens in your kitchen in Oaks Preserve or at the Nokomis beach pavilion with nothing to remember. Being ten minutes away also means we can be flexible: a quick in-person recheck is easy, and a video visit is available for weeks when getting in the car does not make sense.

Frequently asked questions

Do you treat couples together?

Each session is 1-on-1, but couples sometimes book back-to-back appointments and share rides.

Is a free phone consult really free?

Yes. 15 minutes with Dr. Jared to find out whether MD Fit is the right fit before booking an evaluation.

Do you accept Medicare?

MD Fit is cash-pay and does not bill Medicare. We can provide itemized receipts.

Do you take insurance?

No. MD Fit is self-pay, so you get one clinician for the whole visit, long appointments and no visit caps. Some clients submit the receipt to their plan or HSA/FSA themselves; check with your plan.

Where exactly do I park?

In the lot directly in front of the clinic on Clark Road. It is free, and the entrance is at ground level.
