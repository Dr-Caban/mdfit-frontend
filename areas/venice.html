How to get here

From downtown Venice and the Venice Fishing Pier: about 25 minutes north on US-41. From North Venice and Venice East: about 20 minutes. From Nokomis and Casey Key: about 20 minutes. Parking is on-site at Clark Road.

Two routes work. The coastal one is US-41 north from Venice Avenue through Nokomis and Osprey, past Oscar Scherer State Park, to Clark Road, where you turn right; the clinic is a short way along on the north side with a lot in front. The interstate one is Jacaranda Boulevard (exit 193) or Laurel Road (exit 195) to I-75 north, three exits to Clark Road (exit 205), then west about five miles. From Wellen Park and the River Road side the interstate is usually faster; from the island and Venice Gardens, US-41 is more direct. Neither touches downtown Sarasota.

Who we see from Venice

Snowbirds who want to stay strong through the season. Golfers from Plantation Golf and Country Club, Venice Golf and Country Club and Pelican Pointe. Retirees managing chronic back, shoulder and knee pain. Pickleball players from Wellen Park and the Venice courts. Older adults rehabbing after knee and hip replacement.

Seasonal residents

Venice has one of the most seasonal populations on the coast, and snowbirds use the clinic in a recognizable way: an evaluation in the first weeks after arriving, weekly visits through the heart of the season, and a home program in the free MD Fit app to carry north in April. The app holds the videos, reps and progressions, so the routine continues in Ohio or Ontario, and a video visit over the summer keeps the plan moving until you are back on Venice Avenue. Bring imaging or surgical notes from your northern physicians to the first appointment.

What Venice clients come in for

  • Hips and knees that have worn over decades: hip osteoarthritis, knee osteoarthritis, and the outside-of-hip ache of bursitis or a weak gluteus medius.
  • Backs that limit walking distance: spinal stenosis and leg heaviness after a few blocks of the Venetian Waterway or Legacy Trail, tight quadratus lumborum, and upper buttock pain that is often piriformis rather than the spine.
  • Golf and pickleball shoulders: rotator cuff tendon pain, infraspinatus trigger points felt as deep shoulder pain, and the slow stiffening of a frozen shoulder.
  • Feet after a winter of beach walking: plantar fasciitis, arch and inner-ankle pain, and tibialis posterior overload.
  • Balance and fall prevention, often requested by a spouse or adult child, and strength for older adults who want to stay independent at home or on a boat.
  • Cyclists and swimmers from the Legacy Trail and the Gulf with a nagging shoulder or low back.

What we treat

Low back pain, sciatica, neck pain, rotator cuff and shoulder pain, knee and hip pain, chronic pain, post-surgical rehab, sports injury rehab, medical massage, balance and fall prevention, and strength and performance training. The Conditions page has the full list.

Combining the visit

Many Venice clients time their visits around a stop at Siesta Key or St. Armands. We can book your appointment early or late in our 8 AM to 4 PM window to make the day work for you.

Your first visit and what it costs

The evaluation takes about 90 minutes and is $300. Dr. Jared sees you himself for the whole appointment: history, movement assessment, hands-on treatment and the first version of your home plan, which goes into the app before you leave. Follow-up visits are $150 and last about an hour. MD Fit is a self-pay practice: we don't bill insurance, no referral is needed, and you pay at the visit. Some clients submit the receipt to their plan or HSA/FSA themselves; check with your plan. The free phone consult is a short call with Dr. Jared to decide whether a visit is worth the drive, and it is the right first step if you are unsure what is causing the pain.

Frequently asked questions

Do you treat Venice snowbirds for short visits?

Yes. Single-session medical massage or a short course of focused rehab is common for seasonal visitors.

Is this different from physical therapy?

MD Fit is a medical rehabilitation and performance clinic, not a traditional PT practice. Visits are longer, cash-pay and 1-on-1 with Dr. Jared.

Can I bring my golf clubs?

You don't need to. The clinic has what we need to assess and train the swing-related pieces.

Do you take insurance?

No. MD Fit is self-pay, which means one clinician for the whole visit, long appointments and no cap on how many visits your plan allows. Some clients submit the receipt to their plan or HSA/FSA themselves; check with your plan.

Can I keep the program going when I go north for the summer?

Yes. Your exercises stay in the free MD Fit app, and a video visit can update the plan from wherever you are.

Which route from Venice avoids traffic best?

I-75 from Jacaranda or Laurel Road to exit 205 is usually quickest from the east side of Venice and Wellen Park. US-41 is more direct from the island and Nokomis.
