How to get here

From Lakewood Ranch Main Street or Waterside: about 20 minutes south on I-75 to Clark Road, then west. From Lakewood Ranch Blvd neighborhoods: about 20–25 minutes. From University Town Center (UTC): about 15 minutes.

The drive is almost entirely interstate. Join I-75 at University Parkway (exit 213), SR-70 (exit 217) or SR-64 (exit 220), head south past Fruitville and Bee Ridge, and leave at Clark Road (exit 205). Turn right, toward the Gulf, and the clinic is a few minutes along, well before US-41, with a parking lot out front. When I-75 backs up on Friday afternoons in season, Honore Avenue runs south from University Parkway to Clark Road as a surface-street alternative.

Who we see from Lakewood Ranch

Golfers from Lakewood Ranch Golf and Country Club, Country Club East, Esplanade and The Concession. Pickleball players from Premier Sports Campus and private clubs. Working professionals with chronic neck and desk-related pain. Families with teen athletes needing return-to-sport rehab. Retirees who want to stay strong and mobile.

A note for golfers

The Golf Performance program is one of our most-requested. Many Lakewood Ranch golfers come to work on hip mobility, thoracic rotation and the hip-driven piece of their swing. Dr. Jared is a golfer too and understands what the swing demands.

The common pattern in a Lakewood Ranch golfer is a lead hip that does not internally rotate, so the finish is borrowed from the lower back, and four or five rounds a week turn that into one-sided low back pain, SI joint irritation or labral-type pain at the front of the hip. The forearms take the rest: golfer's elbow in the trail arm, tennis elbow in the lead. Treatment releases the TFL, piriformis and thoracic extensors, then trains rotation you can use on the course. No need to bring clubs.

Pickleball, tennis and the courts

Lakewood Ranch has more courts per household than almost anywhere on the Suncoast. In tennis and pickleball players we most often see shoulder impingement from serving, Achilles and soleus strains from sudden stops, plantar fasciitis with a new pair of court shoes, and in newer players a front-of-shoulder ache or a knee that swells after doubles. The plan is almost never to stop playing; it is to fix the mechanics overloading one tissue and build the capacity that was missing.

Desk work, remote work and the commute

Much of Lakewood Ranch works from home or commutes down I-75, and desk workers bring a recognizable set: neck pain worst by late afternoon, headaches from the base of the skull, a knot along the inside edge of the shoulder blade, and a levator scapulae that never switches off. These respond to hands-on release plus a ten-minute daily routine, and they are the cases where a video follow-up works best.

Teens and families

With the Premier Sports Campus and strong school athletics nearby, we see a steady run of high-school athletes after ACL reconstruction, hamstring strains and ankle sprains, usually once formal therapy has ended but the athlete is not yet confident cutting, sprinting or landing. The goal is a tested, measurable return to sport.

How a plan usually runs

The first visit is a 90-minute evaluation ($300) that includes hands-on treatment, not just assessment. Follow-ups are $150 and are typically weekly to start. MD Fit is a self-pay practice: we don't bill insurance, no referral is needed, and you pay at the visit; evaluation $300 for about 90 minutes, follow-ups $150. Everything assigned goes into the free MD Fit app, so the exercises can be done in a garage gym on Lakewood Ranch Boulevard, at a community fitness center or between meetings, with the reps and tempo written down. As the home program takes over, in-person visits space out, and some become video visits. If you would rather talk first, the free phone consult is a 15-minute call with Dr. Jared.

Frequently asked questions

Do you treat kids and teens?

Yes, for sports and post-surgical rehabilitation. Younger children are referred to a pediatric specialist first.

Can I book just a medical massage?

Yes. No evaluation needed. Fill out the short intake and book 60 or 90 minutes.

How often do most Lakewood Ranch clients come?

Many come once a week with a home plan between visits. A standard 6-week program is common for shoulder or hip rehab.

Do you take insurance?

No. MD Fit is self-pay, which is what makes one clinician for the whole visit, long appointments and no visit caps possible. Some clients submit the receipt to their plan or HSA/FSA themselves; check with your plan.

Is there a faster way than I-75?

Not usually. I-75 to exit 205 is the direct route. When the interstate is slow, Honore Avenue south from University Parkway reaches Clark Road without getting on the highway.

Can my spouse or I book back-to-back appointments?

Yes. Many couples from Lakewood Ranch share the drive and book consecutive sessions. Mention it when booking and we will line the times up.
