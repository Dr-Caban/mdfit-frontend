How to get here

From Lakewood Ranch Main Street or Waterside: about 20 minutes south on I-75 to Clark Road, then west. From Lakewood Ranch Blvd neighborhoods: about 20–25 minutes. From University Town Center (UTC): about 15 minutes.

Who we see from Lakewood Ranch

Golfers from Lakewood Ranch Golf and Country Club, Country Club East, Esplanade and The Concession. Pickleball players from Premier Sports Campus and private clubs. Working professionals with chronic neck and desk-related pain. Families with teen athletes needing return-to-sport rehab. Retirees who want to stay strong and mobile.

What we treat

All the conditions on the Conditions page: back, neck, shoulder, knee, hip pain, sciatica, chronic pain, post-surgical rehab, sports injury rehab, medical massage, functional mobility, balance training, and strength and performance training.

A note for golfers

The Golf Performance program is one of our most-requested. Many Lakewood Ranch golfers come to work on hip mobility, thoracic rotation and the hip-driven piece of their swing. Dr. Jared is a golfer too and understands what the swing demands.

Frequently asked questions

Do you treat kids and teens?

Yes, for sports and post-surgical rehabilitation. Younger children are referred to a pediatric specialist first.

Can I book just a medical massage?

Yes. No evaluation needed. Fill out the short intake and book 60 or 90 minutes.

How often do most Lakewood Ranch clients come?

Many come once a week with a home plan between visits. A standard 6-week program is common for shoulder or hip rehab.
