Getting to Clark Road

Clark Road (State Road 72) runs east to west across Sarasota: it begins as Stickney Point Road at the Siesta Key south bridge, crosses US-41, passes the clinic and ends at I-75 exit 205. That makes the directions simple from any direction. From the north (Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, University Parkway) take I-75 south to exit 205 and go west, or follow US-41 south through Sarasota. From the south (Osprey, Nokomis, Venice) take US-41 north and turn right at Clark Road, or I-75 north to exit 205. From the keys, cross at Stickney Point and continue east. Parking is in the lot in front of the building and the entrance is at ground level.

Drive times at a glance

  • Siesta Key: about 10 minutes. Osprey: 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Downtown Sarasota, Palmer Ranch and UTC: about 15 minutes.
  • Lakewood Ranch and Nokomis: about 20 minutes.
  • Venice, Bradenton and Longboat Key: 20 to 30 minutes depending on the route and the season.

Visiting from farther away

Seasonal residents and visitors from outside these towns are welcome. A first visit is a 90-minute evaluation ($300) that includes treatment; follow-ups are $150, and a free phone consult is available before you commit to a drive. Because the exercise program lives in the free app and video visits can cover some follow-ups, clients from Englewood, North Port, Parrish, Anna Maria Island and out of state can usually keep a plan going with fewer trips to Clark Road. MD Fit is self-pay and does not bill insurance; no referral is needed.
