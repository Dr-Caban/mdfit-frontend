Where we are

3214 Clark Road, Sarasota, FL 34231. On Clark Road between US-41 and I-75, easy to reach from Siesta Key (about 10 minutes), downtown Sarasota (about 15 minutes), Palmer Ranch and Osprey (about 15 minutes), and Lakewood Ranch (about 20 minutes). Plenty of parking on-site.

Clark Road is State Road 72: it starts as Stickney Point Road at the south bridge to Siesta Key, crosses US-41, passes the clinic and ends at I-75 exit 205. From the interstate, exit 205 and head west about five miles; from the beach, cross Stickney Point and keep going east past US-41. The lot is directly in front of the building, on the ground floor.

  • Downtown, Rosemary District and the bayfront: US-41 south about 15 minutes, or Tuttle or Beneva Avenue to Clark if US-41 is slow near the hospital.
  • Gulf Gate, South Gate and the Bee Ridge corridor: under 10 minutes by Beneva, Swift or McIntosh Road.
  • Palmer Ranch: north on Honore Avenue or McIntosh Road to Clark Road, about 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Fruitville Road, east Sarasota, Lido and Longboat Key: I-75 south to exit 205, or the Ringling Causeway and US-41 south, 15 to 25 minutes.

Who we see in Sarasota

Downtown desk workers with low back and neck pain. Pickleball and tennis players from Payne Park, Bath & Racquet Club and Siesta Beach. Golfers from Bobby Jones Golf Club, The Oaks and Laurel Oak. Snowbirds who want to stay strong through the season. Older adults recovering from knee and hip replacement. Runners training for the Sarasota Half Marathon.

Many of the remote and office workers we see spend the week at a laptop and the weekend on a court, a bike or a boat. Seasonal residents arrive in November out of shape and play five days a week by January. Retirees downtown and on the keys mostly want to keep walking the bayfront, the Legacy Trail and the beach without a hip or knee deciding for them.

What Sarasota bodies tend to bring in

  • Pickleball and tennis: shoulder impingement, tennis elbow, calf strains and Achilles pain, often starting as a dull ache at the front of the shoulder or lower calf.
  • Golf: low back pain on the lead side, golfer's elbow, and stiff hips that put the load on the quadratus lumborum and SI joint.
  • Running and the Legacy Trail: plantar fasciitis, IT band pain, shin splints, and a weak glute medius felt as outside-of-hip pain.
  • Desk and driving: desk neck, tension headaches from the suboccipitals and upper traps, and pain between the shoulder blades. Cyclists and swimmers bring the same upper-back pattern from volume.
  • After surgery: knee replacement, hip replacement and rotator cuff repair recovery once the surgeon clears active rehab.

Local referrers

Dr. Jared works alongside orthopedic surgeons, primary care doctors and pelvic-floor specialists across Sarasota County. If you have a surgical date on the calendar, bring the paperwork to your first visit and we'll build the plan around your surgeon's precautions.

What a first visit looks like

The first appointment is an evaluation of about 90 minutes, $300: history, movement assessment, hands-on treatment the same day, and a written plan with a short video list in the free MD Fit app. Follow-up sessions are $150 and run about an hour. MD Fit is a self-pay practice: we don't bill insurance, no referral is needed, and you pay at the visit; evaluation $300 for about 90 minutes, follow-ups $150. A free phone consult comes first if you are not sure the clinic is the right fit.

Homework in the app, wherever you live in town

Most progress happens between appointments. The free MD Fit app holds the exact exercises assigned at your visit, filmed by Dr. Jared with sets, reps and tempo written out, so the routine works in a living room, a condo gym downtown or on the beach at Siesta. Visits are usually weekly at first, then every two or three weeks, and a video visit can replace an in-person follow-up once the plan is mostly progression.

Frequently asked questions

Do you take insurance?

No. MD Fit is self-pay, which is what makes one clinician for the whole visit, long appointments and no visit caps possible. Some clients submit the receipt to their plan or HSA/FSA themselves; check with your plan.

Is parking easy?

Yes. There is dedicated parking at the clinic.

Do you treat visitors to Sarasota?

Often. Snowbirds and vacationers book single sessions all winter. Bring any surgical paperwork or imaging reports you have.

How long is the drive from Siesta Key in season?

Usually 10 to 15 minutes. The Stickney Point bridge leads straight onto Clark Road, so you avoid US-41 almost entirely.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

No. Book directly. If you have imaging or notes from a Sarasota physician or surgeon, bring them.
