What makes it clinical

Every program starts with a movement and strength assessment: squat, hinge, press, pull, carry, single-leg work. We identify the limits and the goals, then build sessions that address both. Loads are prescribed, not guessed.

Who we train

First-time lifters who want to learn right. Adults returning to the gym after years off or an injury. Golfers, pickleball players, runners and tennis players who want sport-specific strength. People in their 60s, 70s and 80s who want to stay strong for life.

A typical session

45 to 60 minutes of focused work: a short mobility prep, two or three main lifts with coaching, accessory work, and a conditioning finisher if appropriate. You leave with a plan for your other training days in the week.

Equipment

Full gym access on-site: barbells, dumbbells, cables, kettlebells, sled, rings and more. Nothing you need to bring except shoes and water.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I train?

Two to four sessions a week works for most people. Many clients do one or two with Dr. Jared and the rest on their own with the written plan.

Do you program for home workouts?

Yes. Many clients train once a week in-clinic and follow a home or commercial-gym plan the rest of the week.

Can I combine this with rehab?

That's what MD Fit is built for. The strength plan picks up where rehab leaves off, in the same clinic with the same clinician.
