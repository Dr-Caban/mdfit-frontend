What medical massage is

Medical massage is clinical soft-tissue work, not a spa service. The goal is specific: find the muscles that reproduce your pain or restriction and release them with precise pressure and technique. It is often paired with movement and breathing cues so the change holds after you leave.

What a session looks like

You arrive, change if needed, and share the one or two areas that are bothering you most. Dr. Jared tests how you move and locates the muscles that reproduce your symptoms, then uses hands-on technique on those tissues. Toward the end you may do a few resets so the body relearns the new range. You leave with a short home routine in the MD Fit app.

60 or 90 minute options

60-minute sessions work well for one area such as neck and shoulders, low back, or hips. 90-minute sessions are better if you have two areas to treat or want more thorough work on a stubborn spot. The intake form helps us pick the right length when you book.

Who it's for

Desk workers with chronic neck and shoulder tension. Athletes recovering from a hard training week. People with sticky low-back or hip tightness. Anyone with muscle guarding after an injury or surgery. If you are unsure whether massage or a full rehabilitation evaluation is right for you, book the free phone consult and we will point you in the right direction.

What makes it different

It is done by a licensed massage therapist who is also a physician and clinical exercise physiologist. That means the hands-on work connects to a bigger picture of how your body moves, loads and recovers.
