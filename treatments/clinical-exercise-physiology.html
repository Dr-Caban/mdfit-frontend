What a CEP does

CEPs prescribe exercise for people with cardiovascular, metabolic, pulmonary, neurological and musculoskeletal conditions. We know when it is safe to push, when to hold, and how to progress someone who hasn't exercised in years because of a diagnosis.

Who it's for

People with high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, controlled coronary artery disease, obesity, osteoporosis, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, long COVID, or any condition that has made exercise feel uncertain.

What's different about the first visit

A full history, resting vitals, movement screen and conservative loading. We take notes your other providers can use. If anything suggests you should be cleared by your physician first, we will tell you.

Communication with your care team

With your permission, we send a short summary to your doctor so your care is coordinated.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?
