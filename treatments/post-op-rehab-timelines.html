Knee replacement (TKA)

Weeks 0–2: pain control, range of motion, swelling management. Weeks 2–6: walking distance, stair training, early strength. Weeks 6–12: full strength, balance, return to activity. Many clients are back to walking groups, golf and pickleball by month three with the right plan.

Hip replacement (THA)

Weeks 0–4: safe mobility and transfers, early glute activation. Weeks 4–8: strength, gait, stairs. Weeks 8–16: return to activity. Most clients are back to full daily life and recreation by month four.

Rotator cuff repair

Phase 1 (weeks 0–6): sling, passive range, posture resets. Phase 2 (weeks 6–12): active range, scapular control. Phase 3 (months 3–6): progressive strengthening. Phase 4 (months 6+): return to overhead work and sport. Timelines depend on the size of the repair.

ACL reconstruction

Months 0–3: range, early strength, light cardio. Months 3–6: strength base, running progression, change of direction. Months 6–9: sport-specific training. Return-to-sport testing before full competition.

Spine procedures (laminectomy, fusion, microdiscectomy)

Weeks 0–6: walking program and gentle mobility, strict adherence to surgeon precautions. Weeks 6–12: core and hip strength, gradual loading. Months 3–6: return to lifting, work and recreation.
