Why chronic pain is different

After months of pain, the nervous system becomes more sensitive and movement patterns start to protect painful areas. The fix isn't just finding the sore muscle: it's rebuilding confidence that movement is safe.

The first visit

A full history (including sleep, stress, past medical events), a movement screen, and gentle hands-on testing. We often leave the first visit with one or two things that reproduce your pain and one or two things that already feel better. That becomes the plan.

The weekly structure

Most clients come once or twice a week for the first month, then taper. Sessions combine hands-on soft-tissue work, mobility, breathing retraining and gradual loading. Home work is kept short and realistic.

When to refer out

If anything suggests the pain needs imaging, medication review or a different specialist, Dr. Jared tells you and refers out. MD Fit works alongside your medical team.

Frequently asked questions

Will I ever be pain-free?

Many chronic-pain clients become pain-free or close to it. Some reduce pain enough to live well again. Your evaluation sets realistic expectations.

Is this for fibromyalgia?

Many fibromyalgia clients benefit from gentle loading and nervous-system work. We go at the pace you can handle.

Do I need to try medications first?

No. Medication decisions stay with your physician. MD Fit focuses on movement and soft-tissue care.
