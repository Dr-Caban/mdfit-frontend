What earns

Subscriptions, every month

You earn on what a member you referred actually pays for these plans, each time they are charged.

    Plans and services that are care from Dr. Jared do not earn a share. Florida law does not allow a healthcare provider to pay for patient referrals, whatever the payment is called, so that line is built into the system and cannot be switched on by mistake.
