# MD Fit: every page and what it is for

A plain list of every HTML page on www.mymdfit.com, who it is for and what it does. Written Nov 21, 2026.

This file lives in `docs/`, which is never published to the website (`.vercelignore`). It names staff-only and private pages, so it
should not be moved to the site root, where anyone could read it.

**How pages become addresses.** The site drops ".html", so `book.html` is opened at `/book`. Two addresses point to another file:
`/admin` opens `workspace.html`, and `/developer` opens `dev.html`. Old addresses redirect to new ones: `/privacy` → `/privacy-policy`,
`/evaluation` → `/book?type=eval`, `/our-services` → `/treatments`, and a few more in `vercel.json`.

**Labels used below.**
- **Public**: anyone can open it, and search engines list it.
- **Private link**: anyone with the address can open it, but search engines are told not to list it, and the site doesn't link to it.
- **Sign-in**: shows nothing useful until the person signs in.
- **Staff**: for Dr. Jared and his team only. The server refuses everything on it without a staff sign-in.
- **EMR**: touches patient records. These are described here, never changed by this file. They are locked; see "DO NOT TOUCH" in `README.md`.

## 2. Member pages (sign-in)

| Page | Address | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| `login.html` | `/login` | Sign in, create an account, reset a password, or sign in with Google or Apple. Sign-up asks "How do you want to train?" (on your own, with a trainer, or with a clinician) and takes an optional referral code. In the Home Screen app it shows sign-in only, with no site menu. |
| `account.html` | `/account` | **The MD Fit member app.** Everything a member does lives here, in tabs: Home, Train (workouts, programs, videos, Pain Tool), Progress, Profile and More, plus Solomon (the guide), the first-open tour, rewards, challenges and sharing. Its look comes from `app-theme.css`, which is generated. |
| `calendar-add.html` | `/calendar-add` | Adds a member's visits, classes, billing and subscription dates to their phone's calendar. |
| `app-return.html` | `/app-return` | After paying on Stripe or managing billing, this page reopens the MD Fit phone app ("Taking you back to the app…"), with a button in case nothing happens and an option to stay on the website. |
| `shared.html` | `/shared` | **Private link.** What someone sees when a member shares something: a saved workout or program (`?s=`), one of Dr. Jared's programs (`?p=`), or a Pain Tool protocol (`?m=`). Anyone can read it; joining from it credits the sharer's referral code. |
| `pro.html` | `/pro` | **Private link, sign-in.** The portal for trainers and clinicians. They apply here with their own member account. Once Dr. Caban approves them, they see the members who chose them, assign MD Fit programs, protocols and library workouts, and, for clinicians only, a member's pain check-ins while that member allows it. Never any EMR data. |

## Keeping this list right

- **New page:** add it to the matching section with its address and one line on what it's for.
- **Generated pages** (the local landing pages and every library folder): only the counts here change; describe a new folder, not each page.
- **EMR pages** (`workspace.html`, `admin-classic.html`, `intake.html`, `visit.html`): this file only describes them. Any change to them follows the "DO NOT TOUCH" rules in `README.md`.
- For the systems behind the pages (hosting, the API, keys, email, payments), see `docs/SYSTEM-INDEX.md` and `docs/HANDOFF.md`.
