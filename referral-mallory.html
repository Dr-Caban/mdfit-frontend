Moving from Jane

A slow, safe migration. Nothing is switched off until you're ready.

  1. You request your Jane exportJane releases bulk exports to the account owner. Chart exports can take 1–2 weeks, so we start early.
  2. We import your clients, charts and filesEach matched to the right client profile, then checked with you.
  3. Both systems run side by sideYou keep Jane until you're confident everything is in place.
  4. Weekly encrypted exports for the first 3 monthsA complete copy of your records sent to you every week, so your data is always in your hands.
  5. You switch overOn your timeline. Jane exports stay with you for your records.

What owning it means

  1. Nothing upfrontNo build fee.
  2. One small monthly shareA percentage of monthly net revenue covering hosting, security, maintenance and updates. Rate and definitions to agree.
  3. Your data is always yoursExport everything at any time, in standard formats.
  4. If you ever leave, you take it with youYour site, your records and your content are handed over. Handover terms to agree.

Before any patient data moves, we sign a HIPAA business associate agreement, and your records are hosted only with providers that sign one too.
