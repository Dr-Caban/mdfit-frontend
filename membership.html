Frequently asked questions

Do I need to live near Sarasota?

No. The app and the remote sessions work anywhere in the United States. If you are near Sarasota you can also see Dr. Jared in person at the clinic on Clark Road.

Is the app free?

Yes, the MD Fit app is free to download and use for the pain tool and the exercise videos Dr. Jared assigns. A membership unlocks every premade program, the full lecture library, the monthly live Q&A and the progress tools; the Premium plans add scheduled remote sessions.

How do remote sessions work?

Premium plans include scheduled 30-minute remote sessions with Dr. Jared each month, plus direct messaging. He builds a custom program for you and adjusts it as you progress.

Is this a replacement for seeing a doctor?

No. MD Fit is exercise-based rehabilitation and coaching. It works alongside your medical care, and Dr. Jared will tell you when something needs a specialist.

Do you take insurance?

No. MD Fit is a self-pay practice and does not bill insurance. You pay at the visit and receive an itemized receipt; no referral is needed. An evaluation is $300 (about 90 minutes) and follow-ups are $150. Paying directly is what lets Dr. Jared spend a full session with you, with no visit caps set by an insurer.
