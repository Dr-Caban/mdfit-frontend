Common causes we see

  • Prolonged computer and phone use
  • Repetitive gripping and typing
  • Tight scalene and pec muscles that pinch the nerve higher up
  • Pregnancy and hormonal changes
  • Diabetes and thyroid conditions

How MD Fit treats carpal tunnel & wrist pain

  1. Assess. A hands-on evaluation of how you move and which muscles reproduce your pain.
  2. Release. Targeted soft-tissue work on the muscle at the source.
  3. Correct. Mobility and movement fixes so the problem stops coming back.
  4. Assign homework. Short daily video routines in the free MD Fit app.
  5. Strengthen. Progressive strength work so you stay pain-free and keep doing what you love.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need surgery?

Many mild to moderate cases improve without surgery. Severe or long-standing cases may still need it; we work with your doctor.

Should I wear a wrist brace at night?

A neutral wrist splint at night helps many people while the plan takes hold.

What tests do I need?

A clinical exam often shows what's happening. Nerve conduction testing is used for severe or atypical cases through your doctor.
