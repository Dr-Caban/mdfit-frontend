Frequently asked questions

Is all leg pain sciatica?

No. True nerve irritation and muscle-referred pain can feel alike. The evaluation separates them, and anything that needs a medical referral is flagged.

When should I see a doctor urgently?

Numbness in the groin, loss of bladder or bowel control, or rapidly worsening leg weakness need emergency care right away. Call 911.

What does treatment involve?

Release of the muscles that reproduce your pain, mobility work for the hips and spine, and progressive strengthening so the pain does not return.
