Frequently asked questions

Do I need an MRI?

Not for most cases. If there are red flags (progressive weakness, bowel or bladder symptoms, saddle numbness), we send you for urgent care right away.

Should I avoid bending?

Not forever. Early on we find the positions that feel better and avoid the ones that flare you. Over time you earn back full movement with strength.

Will the disc heal?

Many disc herniations shrink or get reabsorbed on their own over months, and most people become pain-free with good rehab.
