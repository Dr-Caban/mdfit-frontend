Common causes we see

  • Tight or weak quadriceps
  • Hip weakness letting the knee cave in
  • Calf tightness changing how you load the knee
  • Recovery after ACL, meniscus or knee replacement surgery
  • Running and sports overuse

How MD Fit treats knee pain & knee surgery recovery

  1. Assess. A hands-on evaluation of how you move and which muscles reproduce your pain.
  2. Release. Targeted soft-tissue work on the muscle at the source.
  3. Correct. Mobility and movement fixes so the problem stops coming back.
  4. Assign homework. Short daily video routines in the free MD Fit app.
  5. Strengthen. Progressive strength work so you stay pain-free and keep doing what you love.

Frequently asked questions

Do you help after knee replacement?

Yes. Rehab rebuilds range of motion, strength and confidence walking, stairs and daily activities.

Can I avoid surgery?

Some knee pain improves fully with the right strengthening. Your evaluation tells you what to expect; surgical questions stay with your surgeon.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and any surgical notes or imaging reports you have.
