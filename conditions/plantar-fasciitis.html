Sharp heel pain with the first steps in the morning is the classic sign of plantar fasciitis. The arch of your foot and the band on the bottom of it get overloaded, usually because the calves, ankles and hips above them are tight or weak. Common causes we see

  • Tight calves and Achilles tendon
  • Stiff ankle joints that force the arch to overwork
  • Weak hip and glute muscles that change how you load the foot
  • Standing jobs, long walks on hard surfaces or new running loads
  • Rapid increase in training, new shoes or sudden weight change

How MD Fit treats plantar fasciitis & heel pain

  1. Assess. A hands-on evaluation of how you move and which muscles reproduce your pain.
  2. Release. Targeted soft-tissue work on the muscle at the source.
  3. Correct. Mobility and movement fixes so the problem stops coming back.
  4. Assign homework. Short daily video routines in the free MD Fit app.
  5. Strengthen. Progressive strength work so you stay pain-free and keep doing what you love.

Frequently asked questions

How long will it take to feel better?

Many people notice a difference in two to four weeks of consistent calf, foot and hip work. Longer cases take longer. Your plan tells you what to expect from your evaluation.

Do I need orthotics or a boot?

Sometimes short-term support helps. Dr. Jared tells you when it is useful and when it slows recovery, and refers out for custom orthotics or a boot when they are truly needed.

Can I keep walking and exercising?

Usually yes. Your plan swaps high-impact loads for ones that let the foot heal, then adds impact back as the pain settles.
