Dr. Jared Caban, MD, MSc, CEP, CCEP, LMT

★★★★★ 5.0 · 89 Google reviews

Dr. Jared Caban leads every plan at MD Fit on Clark Road in Sarasota. He combines medical training, clinical exercise physiology and hands-on medical massage, so the person who works out why something hurts is the same person who treats it and builds the plan that keeps it from coming back.

Training and credentials

  • MD and MSc. Medical training and a master's degree, which shape how he assesses a problem and when he sends someone to another clinician first.
  • CEP and CCEP. Clinical Exercise Physiologist, and the advanced Certified Clinical Exercise Physiologist credential, both through the American College of Sports Medicine. A CEP uses exercise to prevent and manage chronic disease and designs programs for people whose medical history makes exercise more complex than going to the gym and figuring it out. He explains the credential in What is a Clinical Exercise Physiologist?
  • LMT. Licensed Massage Therapist, so hands-on soft-tissue work is part of the same visit rather than a separate referral.

How he works

Every plan at MD Fit follows the same five steps, and Dr. Jared runs each of them himself:

  1. Assess. A hands-on evaluation of how you move and which muscles reproduce your pain.
  2. Release. Targeted soft-tissue work on the muscle at the source.
  3. Correct. Mobility and movement fixes so the problem stops coming back.
  4. Assign homework. Short daily video routines in the free MD Fit app.
  5. Strengthen. Progressive strength work so you stay out of pain and keep doing what you love.

The starting point is usually the source rather than the spot that hurts. Many pains are referred from a muscle somewhere else, which is why the site has a full muscle library showing where each one sends its pain.

What he helps with

Rehabilitation after injury and surgery, chronic and persistent pain, medical massage, clinical exercise for people with medical conditions, functional mobility, strength training, and balance and fall prevention. The conditions and treatments pages go through each in detail, and the activities pages cover training for specific sports and stages of life.

When something is outside what movement and soft-tissue work can address, he says so and points you to the right physician or specialist. That is part of the job, not a failure of it.
